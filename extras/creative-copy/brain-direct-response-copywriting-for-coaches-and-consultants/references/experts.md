# Experts mined

35 videos across 25 channels. Selection was hand-curated against the brief —
evidence, conversion, intrigue, niche and branding, and a cutthroat
problem-to-solution register — after the automatic scorer's output was rejected
for surfacing beginner and off-genre material ahead of working operators.

"Hidden gem" here means low view count with high substance, not low quality.
Several of the sharpest sources in this brain have under 1,000 views.

---

## The working operators

**Stefan Georgi** — *hidden gem by views, A-list by reputation.*
Positioned on the call as "the number one copywriter in the world right now,"
judged on the hardest test: offers that convert on cold traffic. Creator of the
RMBC method. Works health, survival, biz-op and personal development.
Stated results: beat a V-Shred control by 72% rewriting only the lead, taking the
offer from 10–20 sales a day to ~1,500; a story-driven, zero-statistic BioHarmony
lead hitting 70–100 sales a day within weeks, 6% Facebook conversion, 5% email
conversion and a $550 EPC on a Mercola blast; paid $63,000 for a single two-page
lead rewrite under a $3,000-per-point performance deal.
**Uniquely adds:** the anatomy of why a lead fails, broken into five named,
fixable mistakes. The most directly actionable material in the brain.

**Sabri Suby** — *the cutthroat register, stated outright.*
Founder-operator who started in phone sales at 16, split-testing verbal scripts
hundreds of times a day before applying the same principles to written ads.
Coined "value-based marketing." Reports ~20-minute average time on site on his own
long-form book landing page across millions of views.
**Uniquely adds:** the argument that branding is subordinate to measurable return,
and the discipline that an ad's only job is to sell the click. Also the clearest
statement in the corpus that the classic canon needs modifying for a market whose
skepticism starts at 100%.

**Roy Furr** — *hidden gem, four videos, consistently excellent.*
Publisher of *Breakthrough Marketing Secrets*, creator of High Velocity
Copywriting, mentored directly by Clayton Makepeace.
**Uniquely adds:** the Sequence of Beliefs, the ABC headline test, the three
big-idea types, and the hook/headline/lead distinction. Also the strongest
statement of the research-over-wordsmithing position: "you want to beat the
control? Do the math."

**Kyle Milligan (Copy Squad)** — *the teardown specialist.*
Colour-codes real sales letters live. States a Wealth Press launch he ran cleared
roughly $800,000 in a few days against a prior in-house record of $330,000.
**Uniquely adds:** the only complete, move-by-move structural walkthrough of a
working sales letter in the corpus, plus Nesby as a line-by-line audit tool rather
than a headline formula.

**Alex Cattoni (Copy Posse)** — *three videos, the most systematic.*
Runs the Copy Posse Launch Pad.
**Uniquely adds:** end-to-end sales page anatomy as a checklist, and the only
treatment of document formatting — font sizes, heading hierarchy, readability
checks, mobile line-wrap — as a conversion lever in its own right.

**Joanna Wiebe** — *founder of the discipline.*
Coined "conversion copywriting" while on the growth team at Intuit and consulting
at Conversion Rate Experts.
**Uniquely adds:** the only research-first method here. Assemble from voice-of-
customer data rather than drafting from instinct, and the read-aloud eyes-closed
test for whether copy is specific enough. Also the distinction between what is
safe to claim universally on a homepage and what is safe in one targeted email.

**Harry Dry (Marketing Examples, via David Perell)** — *the craft pole.*
100,000+ newsletter subscribers with unusually high reply engagement.
**Uniquely adds:** the three-question test, the zoom-in method for getting from
abstract to concrete, and a fully narrated real-time rewrite — two days, roughly
25 versions — showing how a line actually evolves. Writes and designs
simultaneously in the real medium rather than drafting in a plain doc.

**Jon Benson** — *two videos, VSL mechanics.*
Veteran direct-response copywriter specialising in NLP-based persuasion.
Reports +$40,000 in 5 days from a 4-U headline rework on a few thousand
visitors, at a $469 price point converting around 5%; adding urgency alone was
worth a full conversion point.
**Uniquely adds:** open loops — how to open, nest and close them, and the recall
language that closes one.

**Anna Hickman** — *live build.*
States over $30k/month in digital product sales, and a year of studying
copywriting two hours a day.
**Uniquely adds:** a complete sales page written live for a real client, section
by section, mapped onto a StoryBrand-style script — you see the drafting, not just
the advice.

**Jesse Forrest** — *twenty years in, dissecting his own page.*
Copywriter since 2004; "hundreds of clients in over 103 different industries."
**Uniquely adds:** an 18-section page skeleton with the reasoning behind each
choice, and the sharpest material on refund prevention through takeaway selling.

**Craig Garber** — *thinner than hoped.*
Publisher of the *Seductive Selling* newsletter. The video mined is a light Q&A
rather than a deep lecture, so it contributes less on emotional drivers than the
brief wanted. Contributes the swipe-file compass test and the rule that an email
must never make the reader click through to finish the story.

**Megan Kachigan (via paidcopywriter)** — *the coaching niche, specifically.*
Direct response copywriter niched into coaches, course creators and personal
brands, writing throughout the funnel. Former physics teacher.
**Uniquely adds:** how client work in this niche is actually won — relationships,
groups, masterminds and referrals rather than cold pitching — and the finding that
copywriters lose these clients by going quiet, not by writing badly.

**Daniel Priestley** — *the funnel outlier.*
Co-founder of ScoreApp, which he discloses in the video.
**Uniquely adds:** the only lead-generation mechanic rather than a persuade-and-
close page — an assessment/quiz with a stated 20–40% start rate and a
qualification layer nobody else covers.

**Conversionly** — *VSL structure.*
States the 8-step script scaled the business to $10M within two years.
**Uniquely adds:** the current-generation VSL length guidance — under 5 minutes,
with the deep dive moved to the post-booking page.

**Adam Erhart, Shweta Saxena, Lawton Chiles, Laura Egocheaga, GOLD SCALE** —
supporting sources. Erhart supplies a compact list of 15 named psychological
triggers mapped to tactics. Saxena supplies line-level persuasion phrases and the
only external psychology citation in the corpus. Chiles appears only through
testimonials, one of which reports a 170% landing page conversion lift and $25,000
in seven days from a headline change alone. Egocheaga is thin on copy mechanics
and included for the selling-to-coaches angle. GOLD SCALE condenses Kennedy's
*The Ultimate Sales Letter* into a 19-step process.

## The historical masters (reached through the sources above)

**Dan Kennedy** — sold roughly $150 million from the stage using one six-belief
speech structure for nine or ten years. Teaches a 12-box client intake and 15
beat-the-control tactics drawn from consulting for financial publishers, Miracle
Ear, Guthy-Renker and Domino's, where he interviewed founder Tom Monaghan
directly. Notably says he never beat a Gary Halbert control in five attempts, and
argues copy quality is often secondary to segmentation and offer engineering.
**Read his tactical material against the integrity note in SKILL.md** — part of it
is now legally exposed.

**Dick Benson** — "the dean of testing," founded the first direct-marketing agency
in 1961, author of *Secrets of Successful Direct Mail*, died 1996. Reconstructed
here from his book and archival clippings held by Don Hauptman. Source of the
seven testing rules.

**Don Hauptman** — ten-time winner of the Newsletters Promotion Award, an unbeaten
record. Wrote "Speak Spanish Like a Diplomat," credited with tens of millions in
language-course sales. Source of the testimonial-specificity material.

**John Caples** — the 1929 Phoenix Mutual ad "To men who want to quit work
someday," broken down line by line, and "They laughed when I sat down at the
piano."

**Clayton Makepeace** — Furr's mentor. Source of the founding Sequence of Beliefs
question and the checklist Milligan applies to the Todd Brown letter.

**Michael Masterson** — chief growth strategist at Agora Publishing. Source of the
Rule of One, via the top-100-offers analysis.

**Gary Bencivenga, Claude Hopkins, Gary Halbert, Maxwell Sackheim, David L.
Deutsch, Todd Brown, Evaldo** — cited throughout; see `synthesis.md` for what each
contributes and `quote-library.md` for attribution.
