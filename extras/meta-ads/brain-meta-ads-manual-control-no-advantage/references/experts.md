# Experts Mined — Meta Ads Manual Control (vs Advantage+/Andromeda)

16 creators, 24 usable transcripts. "Popular" = six-figure subscriber/view base; "hidden gem" = low views, high substance (surfaced by engagement scoring, not view count).

## Popular

- **Ben Heath** (3 videos) — the small-budget authority. Angle: trust Meta's delivery, control structure and analysis manually. Unique adds: learning phase is conversion-volume not time; consolidation math (20 conv through 1 ad set vs 5); stat-sig calculator gate; per-second video retention-curve analysis; break-even-as-proof-of-concept reframe. Most directly applicable voice for 2-8k dollars/month.
- **Sam Piliero** (3 videos) — the spend-tier mapper. Angle: pack system inside one prospecting CBO, manual exclusions, staged manual controls. Unique adds: 1x-CPA ad-set minimums with 7-day removal; the "use as a suggestion" retargeting gotcha; the only full ladder of which manual levers unlock at which monthly spend; Rule of 10,000 (10 new ads/week per 10k dollars/month).
- **Professor Charley T (Charley Tichenor)** (3 videos) — the contrarian systems thinker. Angle: anti cost-cap, anti-ABO-testing, pro CBO + dynamic creative; judge everything on profit volume. Unique adds: 3-2-2 method with permutation math; incrementality argument (caps/ASC harvest existing demand); Andromeda 1 control-vs-test architecture; explicit one-ad-set collapse rule for low budgets.
- **Nick Theriot** — the anti-ASC case. Unique add: the set's only controlled same-ads test (manual prospecting 2.91 ROAS vs ASC 2.14), plus the Meta-rep confrontation. His structure is "Advantage+ minus the packaging."

- **Davie Fogarty** — the pro-automation pole, 100M+ dollars spent on own brands. Unique adds: budget pegged to product price (10-30%); 2x-AOV kill threshold; per-unit profit math; account-ban-avoidance checklist; objection advertising.

- **Alex Fedotoff** (2 videos) — the intraday manual scaler (e-comm whale context, sells a tool — weight accordingly). Unique adds: 3-Hour Surf Scaling ladder with midnight reset; creative-as-casting rule (model age = buyer age); separate image vs video campaigns.
