# Paid-traffic landing-page decisions

Help the user understand and improve the page they actually have. Preserve the chosen offer and funnel. This skill's practitioner material supplies design options, not guaranteed conversion lifts or permission to publish.

## Inspect before diagnosing

Establish the page's intended audience, incoming promise, conversion action and current approved offer facts. Reuse supplied context; a visual or copy audit does not require ad-account identifiers. Read the actual page or supplied artifact and distinguish observed defects from inferred causes. Use your own offer document as the source of claims, rather than historical examples in this collection.

Follow the path from arrival through the CTA, form, completion state and intended downstream handling. A low conversion rate can reflect traffic quality, measurement, availability, technical failure or follow-up as well as page content. Do not begin by assuming the hero is responsible.

## Review the decisions that matter

- **Hero:** can the intended visitor understand the offer, relevance and next action? Use the three-question check and headline methods in [the synthesis](references/synthesis.md). Preserve accurate identity, mechanism, price and event logistics where they help the decision. No fixed line or word count guarantees clarity.
- **Proof:** use relevant, permissioned evidence with accurate context. A real result may be anonymized when appropriate; do not invent names, faces, ratings or logos to satisfy a layout. Preserve useful proof while adapting its presentation.
- **CTA and form:** make the action and consequence clear. Choose form placement and fields from intent, necessary qualification and the process, not traffic temperature alone. Retain required information and accessible instructions; test validation, recovery and completion.
- **Layout and media:** inspect mobile and desktop, zoom, keyboard operation, contrast and movement. Choose photo, video, product view or another treatment from the message and actual rendering. A viewport percentage, centered crop or prescribed visual cannot prove usability.
- **Page flow:** give visitors enough information to decide. Headline, benefit, mechanism, objections, process, proof and final action are useful building blocks; reorder or omit them to fit the page. Keep required legal, privacy and navigation functions.
- **Measurement:** distinguish a functional defect from a hypothesis about persuasion. Fix observed breakage directly. For an experiment, define the comparison, outcome, likely effect, observation window and decision rule; a universal traffic/spend/conversion floor does not establish statistical evidence.

Use [technical sources](references/primary-sources.md) for accessible-form and performance checks, and the owning engineering or tracking skill for implementation. A successful submission, analytics event and downstream CRM outcome are different evidence. Test external effects only within the user's authority.

## Apply methods with their limits

The [practitioner reference](references/experts.md) and [synthesis](references/synthesis.md) retain hero, headline, quiz, direct-response and segment-page methods. Sources disagree about form placement, proof quantity, video, chat and CTA choices. Treat each as a candidate for the actual brief, not a settled rule from a vote of creators.

[Quotation candidates](references/quote-library.Captions do not certify exact speech, attribution or commercial results. Verify recordings before publishing exact quotes. Agency-reported lifts and historical attention statistics are not forecasts for the user's page.

Do not invent scarcity, bonuses, guarantees, refunds, privacy assurances or support promises. Follow the actual approved terms and current account policy. Preserve the user's authorized workflow rather than imposing GHL, a particular builder or automatic outbound follow-up.

## Return an actionable audit or build

Lead with the most consequential finding or change. For each material issue, connect observed evidence, user impact, proposed repair and a way to verify it. Label conversion hypotheses and missing evidence. Include the actual revised artifact when implementation is requested, inspect its rendered result and report functional checks separately from measured conversion outcomes.
