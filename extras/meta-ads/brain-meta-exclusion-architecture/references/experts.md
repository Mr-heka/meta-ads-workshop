# Experts Mined: Meta ads exclusion architecture (exclusions, overlap control, funnel sequencing)

Mined 2026-07-05. 12 videos, 12 channels, 3 hidden gems (per the source index built at mining time).

## Ben Heath (popular; 2 videos, the backbone of this brain)

- **Angle:** UK agency owner, large channel, the most directly on-scope material in the set. His retargeting video uses live account breakdown data (the $80K campaign) to prove cold ad sets already spend ~40% warm.
- **Uniquely adds:** the controls-vs-suggestion-zone explanation of WHY exclusions fail under Advantage+ audience, the auction overlap mechanism, the lookback-window caps (90/180/365 days), the exact click path for a hard-constraint warm ad set, and the 50-vs-25 conversions consolidation math.

## Dr. Matt Shiver (hidden gem, score 75.89, top of the source table)

- **Angle:** healthcare-adjacent lead gen operator; ran the only controlled experiment in the set, a $2,500 A/B budget split (Advantage+ with value rules vs broad with strict 25-45 age targeting).
- **Uniquely adds:** hard numbers proving the setups tie (71 vs 72 qualified leads), the one-third-of-spend-is-retargeting-either-way finding, the value-rule recipe (decrease bid 90% outside range), and the wrong-optimisation-event trap.

## LYFE Marketing (popular for its size)

- **Angle:** small-business simplification. Exclusions exist mainly to keep the prospecting campaign purely acquisition; almost everything else should be relaxed, not tightened.
- **Uniquely adds:** the three-reasons framing for custom audiences (retarget, exclude, data), the four audience building blocks, the one-campaign two-ad-set structure, the only-three-hard-controls rule (location, age, gender), and the longer-window retargeting stance.

## Chase Chappell (popular for its size)

- **Angle:** spend-tier operator view. Exclusion and overlap discipline matters most at the mid-tier CBO stage; at scale creative variety inside Advantage+ replaces manual segmentation.
- **Uniquely adds:** the spend-tier ladder (<$30K/month = interest testing + creative testing + separate retargeting; >$30K = product-line broad CBOs with a no-overlap rule; >$500K = Advantage+ only), consistent with his tiering in the manual-control verified base.

## Nexal Media | Ecom (hidden gem, 14 views, top-decile engagement)

- **Angle:** the set's contrarian. Argues against excluding past purchasers (brand recall, social proof comments) and against overlap exclusions inside interest tests.
- **Uniquely adds:** the default rule "no exclusions unless there is a specific logical reason", the promo-fairness exception, and the fair-test argument. Conflicts with the verified base on excluding buyers; we side with the verified base for one-time-purchase offers.

## Chelsea Gardner | Fully Aligned Marketing (small channel, lowest score in the table but on-scope)

- **Angle:** loan-officer/mortgage niche, compliance and reputation suppression.
- **Uniquely adds:** the "always exclude" suppression-list method (do-not-contact CRM records, hostile commenters) and the gating condition nobody else states as plainly: exclusion fields only appear with detailed targeting off, original custom audiences, "Limited" mode.

## Ken Ang Wee Kien (small channel, engagement-penalised in scoring)

- **Angle:** narrow practical UI tutorial.
- **Uniquely adds:** the only coverage of account-level employer-based exclusion (advertising settings > Audience control), including its real limits: matching depends on employees self-listing the workplace on their profiles.

## Paul Chinedu Nnamani (small channel, engagement-penalised in scoring)

- **Angle:** exclusion as an audience-refresh and anti-fatigue lever when launching new prospecting creative.
- **Uniquely adds:** the honest caveat that exclusion reduces rather than eliminates exposure, and the multi-audience exclusion workflow (excluding four customs at once). Caveat: no Advantage+ discussion at all; treats original-audience workflow as the default without saying so.

## Excluded or caveated

- **Phillipdigitals - The Ads Guy** (hidden-gem flag, but flagged thin by the reader): 37-view click-path tutorial for the location exclude toggle. Excluded from synthesis themes; the mechanic is covered by LYFE and Heath.
- **Magic Click Partners** (era-flagged): gambling-niche operator with the most detailed turn-off-Advantage+ click path, but the video predicts its own obsolescence (Meta removing the master toggle, "impossible to disable" soon). Quotes retained under a handle-with-care header; verify the UI live before using the path.
- **Performance Marketer Man** (era-flagged): real-estate lead gen diagnostic on audience overlap. Its "set audience exclusion to 110%" maps to no known Ads Manager control; unverified mechanic. Symptom list plausible but the theme stands on Heath without it.

## Source quality notes

- **Usable sources: 9 of 12** (Heath x2, Shiver, LYFE, Chappell, Nexal, Chelsea Gardner, Ken Ang, Paul Chinedu Nnamani). One thin exclusion (Phillipdigitals), two era-flagged caveats (Magic Click Partners, Performance Marketer Man) used only where corroborated. 9 clears the 8-source floor, but narrowly; say so if asked about depth.
- **Freshness:** all sources passed the <=12-months rule at discovery. No pre-Andromeda material presented as current. Paul Chinedu Nnamani's video simply ignores Advantage+ rather than misrepresenting it; treat its click path as original-audience-mode only.
- **Thin areas of the locked scope:** per-city buyer/attendee exclusion for multi-city event funnels is NOT covered by any source directly; the brain derives it from the general buyer-exclusion consensus plus location hard controls. Audience overlap DIAGNOSIS tooling (Meta's overlap comparison report) was not demonstrated by any source; overlap is covered by mechanism and symptoms only. Funnel-stage sequencing beyond cold/warm (three-plus stage ladders) is thin: only Heath, LYFE and Chappell touch it, at two stages.
- **Vertical skew:** half the set is e-commerce or niche verticals (real estate, mortgage, gambling); lead-gen translation applied through the verified manual-control base.
