## What congruence is

> "It means that every element from the ad itself to the landing page needs to align perfectly with the product that's being promoted" — AOK Marketing, *How to Structure Paid Ad Campaigns That Convert*

> "Adsense refers to the idea that your ad should visually and emotionally resemble your landing page and vice versa" — Joe Sayles, Jr., *The 2 Paid Ad Mistakes That Are Secretly Draining Your Budget*

> "It is matching your creative, whether it's an ad or an organic post to the landing page that you're sending that traffic to all the way through the pre and post purchase retention flows, email and SMS" — Replo, *How Funnel Congruency Boosts Conversions and Lowers Ad Costs*

> "Narrative congruence means that everything on your page tells the same story." — Ben McGarry, *Pages That CONVERT*

> "we're not looking for product market match. We're looking for message market match, and that's how we optimize for conversions." — Ben McGarry, *Pages That CONVERT*
