# Experts Mined: Meta attribution measurement deep (practical incrementality at small spend, CRM reconciliation)

Nine videos mined 2026-07-06. Below: each creator, whether they read as a popular voice or a hidden gem (per the scored source index), their angle, and what they uniquely add. Honest source-quality notes at the bottom.

## Marketing Operators: popular (two videos in the set)
**Angle:** Practitioner roundtable of real DTC operators (Ridge, HexClad-adjacent), the most-viewed and most directly relevant source. Two videos: E051 (why purchase-conversion optimisation stops being incremental) and the Incrementality Playbook (Haus.io geo-lift testing at scale).
**Uniquely adds:** live in-account holdout results with real numbers: 58 tests a year, 40% brand-video lift, the 5x incrementality gap vs Meta-reported, rolling-reach as a ceiling diagnostic, and the single most honest line for our scale: full-funnel effects need ~6 months and can't be measured "as performance market." Also the clearest statement that org culture has moved from peer-review rigour to fast directional reads, which is the permission structure for a small account to skip formal holdouts.

## Lorenzo Pravata: popular voice (agency operator, $40M+ managed on Meta)
**Angle:** Marginal-CPA/marginal-ROAS discipline for brands scaling past $1M/month. Explicitly deprioritises MMM and formal geo holdouts below nine figures.
**Uniquely adds:** the cleanest worked example of blended-vs-marginal in the whole set ($20 blended CPA hiding a $33 incremental CPA), the three cheap ways to estimate incrementality without a geo holdout (budget delta, period comparison, pause-and-observe), the full northstar-metric stack (MER, new-customer MER, incremental new-customer ROAS, contribution margin), and creative-cohort tagging tied to Andromeda entity IDs. His budget-delta method is the most transferable idea to our scale.

## Scalability School: strong-signal (small channel, high engagement)
**Angle:** Two agency operators walking a month-by-month case study of testing and then abandoning Meta's native Incremental Attribution on a $150-200K/month brand.
**Uniquely adds:** the deepest real account of Meta's IA feature: the two-cell conversion-lift design, the March click-definition change that broke it, the three-data-point cross-check rule (Meta in-platform + Triple Whale MTA + product-level sales-to-spend), the finding that IA re-scores the same ~70% net-new audience rather than reaching new people, and a clean subscription/MRR blended-MER cash-flow model for deciding acquisition spend.

## Nitro Commerce: strong-signal (India/APAC media agency)
**Angle:** Reframes the problem as platform data being a closed system; pushes a blended-MER-vs-platform-ROAS reconciliation dashboard.
**Uniquely adds:** the crispest single illustration of the platform-vs-blended gap (Google 5.1x, Meta 4.8x, blended MER flat at 1.7x, net revenue +2.3%) and the channel-rank flip (brand search 0.6x lift, retargeting 0.9x, prospecting 1.8x). Caveat: figures are India retail-media (INR crore, UPI apps, OTT), so the specific tactics don't transfer, only the general principle.

## Lifesight: strong-signal (MMM/experimentation vendor)
**Angle:** The canonical academic framing of incrementality via the eBay natural experiment; contrasts credit-allocation attribution against causal measurement.
**Uniquely adds:** the foundational eBay $50M proof, the precise definition of incrementality (marginal contribution above baseline), the statement that attribution "does not have a mechanism to quantify the baseline," and the two named methodologies (observational/cost-based inference vs controlled experiments). Best source for teaching the concept cleanly.

## Boolean Maths: strong-signal (MTA tool vendor demo)
**Angle:** Shows the mechanics of stitching cross-device journeys to reveal Meta/Google over-crediting.
**Uniquely adds:** the concrete over-claim numbers (Meta 855 orders vs verifiable tracked orders, ~10 touchpoints per order), the click-date-not-order-date rule, the four attribution models with when-to-use guidance, and the standard-vs-enriched overlap jump (under 2% to 23%). Best source for understanding WHY dashboards inflate.

## Imad Huda: hidden gem (small channel, top-decile engagement): EXCLUDED (thin)

**Why excluded:** the only small-spend source, but engages zero with attribution, incrementality, blended metrics or CRM reconciliation. Its advice (trust Advantage+ Shopping, judge on in-platform ROAS only) conflicts with our verified manual-control base on two counts. Retained only as the documented counter-position in synthesis.md; not used for claims or quotes.
