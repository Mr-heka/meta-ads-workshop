## Attribution vs incrementality (the foundation)

> "It's the outcome that's driven exclusively by the initiative or channel or tactic under consentation on top of contributions from your baseline factors." — Lifesight, *Unlock True Marketing ROI: Incrementality Testing Explained (eBay Case Study)*

> "While attribution performs credit allocation to touch points either heristically or algorithmically it does not have a mechanism to quantify the baseline and reporting mentality." — Lifesight, *Unlock True Marketing ROI: Incrementality Testing Explained (eBay Case Study)*

> "how many sales did my ads generate if I exclude the ones that would have occurred anyway and also how does the CPA change when I increase the budget" — Lorenzo Pravata, *We Scale Brands to $1M/Month Using This One Metric*

> "That's all incremental really means, right? It's additional. You would not be getting that outcome or that audience or that conversion if you were not doing that tactic." — Marketing Operators, *The Incrementality Playbook Every Ecommerce Marketer Needs*

> "incremental on is not the same as acquisition and I I mean it's it's going to depend on every brand I think it's very incremental" — Marketing Operators, *E051: Why Meta Purchase Conversion Isn't Enough*

## Meta's native Incremental Attribution — trust but verify

> "one day click is only giving credit to people who click within 24 hours. By default, it is substantially more conservative than incremental attribution. So we don't want to be misled by one day click." — Scalability School, *We Tested Meta's Incremental Attribution on $200K/Month*

> "we get to the end of the test and the results basically say incremental attribution is substantially more incremental than one day click" — Scalability School, *We Tested Meta's Incremental Attribution on $200K/Month*

> "we start to see this deviation from one day click and incremental attribution. ... we ended our test probably at the beginning of April, uh, and we started to roll out the test" — Scalability School, *We Tested Meta's Incremental Attribution on $200K/Month*

> "incremental ROAS and ads manager often lands around 90% of seven day click ROAS when you break it down" — Scalability School, *We Tested Meta's Incremental Attribution on $200K/Month*
