# Placement and format decision methods

This reference rebuilds the practical methods in the inherited 11-transcript collection. Creator associations below come from the original synthesis; they locate perspectives, not authenticated recording metadata or independent consensus. Use [primary sources](primary-sources.md) for platform facts and the actual account for settings. Original text and numerical anecdotes remain in [history](../history/original/references/synthesis.md.original).

## Select delivery for the objective

The automatic/manual distinction is covered by Digital Growth Tutor, Samiksha Creation's and My Online Master. Automatic delivery lets the system allocate among eligible placements. Manual selection expresses a deliberate surface constraint. Decide from the brief, asset fit, audience evidence and any current approved account policy. Do not use a creator preference as authority to change that policy.

For broad delivery, diagnose poor rendering before excluding a surface: an adaptation may solve the problem while preserving eligibility. For a surface-specific message, experiment or contractual restriction, inspect the manual controls and choose the intended destinations. A vertical aspect ratio by itself does not require Reels-only delivery.

Keep audience settings distinct from placements. A tutorial changing both is not evidence that one change requires the other. Neither mode guarantees better lead quality or results.

## Make a placement map for this campaign

Digital Growth Tutor and TravelTractions organize surfaces into Feed, Stories/Reels, in-stream, search and external app/site contexts. Use those categories to ask where the creative appears, which interface surrounds it, and what the viewer can do. Build the concrete list from the current objective and format rather than copying the old 21-placement or six-platform count.

Device, operating-system, connection and skippability controls in old walkthroughs are context-specific candidates. Check availability and the actual product requirement before narrowing eligibility. A tutorial does not establish present API enum support.

## Adapt the asset

The collection describes three complementary approaches:

- **Multiple exports:** Digital Growth Tutor and AdManage AI describe grouping different aspect ratios for different surfaces. Supply versions that resolve an actual rendering need; confirm the saved mapping in preview. A tool demo does not prove the tool is installed or still supports that workflow.
- **Reusable composition:** Michael Diaz describes keeping central content within a square inside a vertical master; Dennis Willeboordse describes protecting a taller central area. Both can reduce adaptation work. Neither is a universal safe zone. Interface overlays, crop anchors, text size and the CTA still need inspection.
- **Placement customization:** Param Digital Marketer describes editing media, crop, trim, thumbnails and overlays for specific placements. Inspect the current editing controls and confirm that the intended version is mapped correctly. Do not assume every field or placement is independently customizable.

Common production canvases include 1080×1920 (9:16), 1080×1350 (4:5) and a square (1:1). These describe geometry; eligibility and recommended dimensions depend on the selected format. Check current specifications before exporting. The source's square-is-retired statement is not an instruction to replace working creative.

If a crop loses a face, demonstration, caption or offer detail, recompose or use another version. Reshoot only when adaptation cannot preserve the intended quality or meaning. An existing horizontal source is not automatically unusable.

## Protect the message in motion

SPLASHmedia, Willeboordse and Diaz emphasize surface-aware composition and captions. Review the opening, moving subject and final CTA, not only a still frame. Keep captions accurate and readable; check sound-on and muted understanding. Captions need not be burned in when another supported accessible treatment fits the deliverable. Audio rights and publication permission follow the actual brief.

Use the available social-safe-zones production reference for geometry, then verify it against the actual preview. Check any automatic creative enhancement for changes to text, images, music or layout. A controlled test should hold unrelated transformations stable; ordinary work can use authorized enhancements after reviewing the result.

TravelTractions' preference for disabling multi-advertiser presentation is a contextual creative choice, not a global account rule.

## Inspect carousels and single-surface work

For a carousel, preview the actual selected format: card order, swipe behavior, text, crop, links and CTA. If the campaign requires a sequential story, verify that it survives the chosen placements. Separate an ad set when the delivery requirement or experiment calls for it, and assess budget allocation and fragmentation. Being in the same campaign does not mean a new ad set has no spending consequence.

For Reels-only work, inspect the current placement options, select the authorized destinations, review expansion or limited-spend controls if offered, and read back the saved settings. Do not copy the old 15-second or 90-second ceiling; use the relevant current format limit. A tutorial's crop prompt does not establish a duration cap.

## Evaluate delivery before prescribing exclusions

Doulgeridis uses placement breakdowns to decide where creative effort may help. Samiksha reports a client improvement after narrowing placement. These are observations and anecdotes, not a controlled general comparison. TravelTractions' demographic figures likewise do not identify this campaign's audience.

Compare spend, delivery, result definitions, qualified leads and downstream outcomes over aligned windows. Account for reporting lag, attribution and different audiences or formats. Delivery share is selected by the system; it does not directly estimate what would happen under a different placement policy. Use the report to locate a problem and define a comparison, rather than calling an entire surface junk.

If evidence supports an authorized exclusion, inspect its scope and save/read back the setting. Later delivery breakdowns can help check enforcement. Do not claim a fixed per-excluded-placement leakage percentage, an API hard block or an account-wide installed rule from the inherited notes.

SPLASHmedia's chunks-of-four method is one way to organize creative variations. Preserve the useful principle of a defined question and interpretable comparison; choose the number of variants from the available evidence, budget and production capacity.

## Return a useful decision

State the intended placements, adaptations required, previews inspected, unresolved specification checks and evidence needed to judge results. Preserve an existing approved account policy until the user authorizes a relevant change.
