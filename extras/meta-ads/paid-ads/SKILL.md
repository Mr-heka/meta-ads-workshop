# Paid ads

Turn the business objective and observed funnel into a channel plan, a measurable test or a supported account decision. Establish current access before claiming account knowledge. A strategy request does not require starting a campaign.

## Establish the decision

Read the relevant project and product-marketing context when present. Use canonical offer and brand sources for their facts. Retain the account, budget, model of attribution and authorization already established in the task. Ask only for missing information that changes the decision; use clearly labelled scenarios for optional unknowns.

Record the desired outcome, geography, time window, available budget, contribution assumptions, audience, existing channels, creative capacity and conversion path. Distinguish a lead, qualified lead, booked meeting, purchase and retained customer. Do not substitute one for another because its count is easier to obtain.

## Work the appropriate phase

1. **Plan:** compare plausible channels against demand, audience reach, creative capacity, measurement and economics. [Planning](references/planning.md) covers channel selection, test capacity and campaign structure.
2. **Measure:** reconcile spend and outcomes from compatible periods and sources. [Measurement](references/measurement.md) defines the offline calculator and interpretation limits.
3. **Diagnose:** locate the evidence gap or funnel change, form alternatives and choose a discriminating check. [Execution](references/execution.md) covers audience, creative, tracking, launch and change handoffs.
4. **Act and verify:** carry out the authorized change through the available platform owner, then inspect the resulting state. Reconcile an uncertain write before retrying. Preserve authorization already granted; hold only an unapproved spending, publication or other consequential decision.

## Return a usable decision

Give the recommendation, supporting account/source/date evidence, budget or test scenario, exact success/stop/review conditions, next action and material unknowns. For implementation include campaign IDs, intended status, actual readback and remaining gates. For an offline plan say what was calculated or reviewed and what account behavior remains unverified.

Examples and failure boundaries are in [decision cases](references/cases.md).
