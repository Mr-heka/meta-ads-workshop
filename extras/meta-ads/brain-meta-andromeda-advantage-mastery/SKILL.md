# Andromeda and Advantage+ decision reference

Use this practitioner collection to plan and evaluate creative and automation choices. It does not establish a universal account setup or authorize spend. Preserve the user's current campaign requirements and approved policy.

## Ground the decision

Identify the business outcome, account constraints, available conversion evidence and creative capacity. Reuse known facts. Separate audience, placement, budget and creative automation; a recommendation for one is not approval for all of them.

[Primary evidence](references/primary-sources.md) distinguishes Meta's documented retrieval architecture from practitioner interpretations. Do not equate Andromeda with the whole delivery system, assume that creative replaces eligibility controls, or claim a mandatory creative count from the engineering article.

## Choose useful methods

Use [the decision reference](references/synthesis.md) for the relevant problem:

- **Structure and audience:** consolidate only where objectives and constraints fit; inspect saved controls versus suggestions before claiming a targeting boundary.
- **Creative planning:** organize concepts by supported audience problems, then choose variations the team can produce and evaluate. Pipeline A/B, angle-format batches and the apparel 4F lens are optional methods.
- **Testing:** choose a control, pack, batch, test-then-scale or supported experiment workflow according to the question. Define the metric, budget authority, observation window and decision criteria. Do not assume ordinary delivery is an even test.
- **Fatigue and pruning:** examine creative response, delivery, tracking and downstream outcomes before naming a cause. Review the aggregate and individual assets; continued spend does not prove an assist role.
- **Signal and economics:** verify what the conversion event represents and reconcile platform reporting with qualification and sales. Revenue minus ad spend is not full profit. Choose scale, hold, reduce or pause from evidence and authorized limits.

Return the proposed choice, why it fits, what evidence supports it, the material trade-off and the next authorized action. The [worked scenarios](examples/brain-meta-andromeda-advantage-mastery-session.md) illustrate audience-boundary and creative-count questions without inventing account facts.
