## What the experts agree on

- **There are six objectives and the objective just names your business goal.** Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App Promotion, Sales, on the first create screen. "a campaign objective is simply the goal that you want your advertisement to achieve" (V.S.L). Backed by V.S.L, Prorecruit, Christian Jamal, Paul Chinedu Nnamani, Edunexia.
- **Objective choice tells Meta which behaviour segment to hunt.** Meta has pre-sorted users into clickers, buyers, form-fillers, likers; the objective picks the bucket (Christian Jamal, Paul Chinedu Nnamani, Edunexia).
- **The #1 mistake is objective/goal mismatch.** Want sales, pick Traffic, get cheap clicks and conclude "the ad isn't working" (V.S.L, Paul Chinedu Nnamani). It repeats one level down at the optimisation goal (Christian Jamal).
- **Buying type defaults to auction; leave it there.** "Facebook's ad algorithm works on auction basis... by default you have to leave it at this" (My Online Master; confirmed Prorecruit). Reservation is the niche brand-buy alternative.
- **The objective is locked once you publish.** To change it you rebuild the campaign (My Online Master).
- **The optimisation/performance goal is a separate lever inside the objective.** It decides what event the auction optimises delivery toward. Under a conversion objective, optimise to the money event, never a cheaper proxy (My Online Master, Digital Growth Tutor, Christian Jamal).
- **For our funnel: Leads when the sale closes offline, Sales only when the whole purchase is online** (Christian Jamal, Paul Chinedu Nnamani; My Online Master for the e-comm Sales case).

## Named frameworks & methods

- **The four-bucket mistake model (Christian Jamal).** Objective failures span "the objective itself, the performance goal, the tracking, and the optimization event." This brain owns bucket one; the optimization event is `brain-meta-optimisation-event-strategy`, tracking is `brain-meta-pixel-capi-signals`.
- **The 50-purchase-events rule (Christian Jamal).** "The sales objective needs about 50 purchase events per week per ad set just to exit the learning phase." Offline-closing service businesses never feed that data back, so they should run Leads.
- **Sales-vs-Leads funnel test (Christian Jamal + Paul Chinedu Nnamani).** Whole purchase online, no human follow-up (e-comm, direct buy button, low-ticket) = Sales. Sale closes on a call or in a room = Leads.
- **"Business owners only ever run Lead or Sales" (Paul Chinedu Nnamani).** Awareness/Traffic/Engagement/App Promotion are for bloggers, big brands, app companies, not direct response.
- **Link click vs landing page view (My Online Master, Digital Growth Tutor).** A link click registers even if the page never loads; a landing page view requires the page to load. Optimise to landing page views over clicks for real traffic.
- **Objective one-liners (Prorecruit, V.S.L).** Awareness optimises to reach / impressions / ad recall lift; Traffic finds clickers for a destination; Engagement chases interactions/messages; Leads captures contact info; Sales finds immediate buyers via catalog/pixel.
- **Real number:** Florida home-service company, correct objective (Leads), "over a thousand leads and over $442,000 in revenue" by month two, past $1M attributed revenue by month five (Christian Jamal).

## Contrarian / disputed takes

- **Sales vs Leads emphasis (contested, cleanly reconciled).** My Online Master: "if you want orders, you must choose Sales... No one else has to go with any objective." Christian Jamal and Paul Chinedu Nnamani: most service businesses should run Leads. Reconciliation: My Online Master is walking a real online checkout, which is exactly the case the others carve out for Sales. No real disagreement, only different default customers.
- **"Andromeda means targeting no longer matters" (conflicts with our verified base, base wins).** My Online Master and Digital Growth Tutor describe Advantage+ Audience as the new default and audience selection as largely overridden. The manual-control doctrine (`brain-meta-ads-manual-control-no-advantage`) says otherwise: switch to Original Audience. Notably both step-by-step creators independently make the same manual choice, so the conflict is only in framing. Keep manual audience control if that is what you have chosen.

## Execution playbook

**IF/THEN operating rules**

- **IF the goal is a purchase completed entirely online (course buy button, e-comm checkout) THEN pick Sales and optimise to Purchase** (Christian Jamal, My Online Master). Needs the pixel firing a Purchase event.
- **IF the sale closes offline (call booked, seat sold, room, DM close) THEN pick Leads, not Sales** (Christian Jamal). Meta can never see the purchase, so Sales starves.
- **IF you want brand reach for a launch or new market THEN Awareness**, and optimise to reach or ad recall lift, not clicks (Prorecruit, V.S.L). Expect no immediate sales.
- **IF you just need site visits THEN Traffic, optimise to landing page views, not link clicks** (My Online Master, Digital Growth Tutor).
- **IF someone reports "lots of clicks/likes, no sales" THEN check for objective/goal mismatch first** (V.S.L, Paul Chinedu Nnamani), then check the optimisation goal isn't a proxy like "maximize link clicks" under Leads (Christian Jamal).
- **IF on the campaign-details screen THEN leave buying type on Auction** (My Online Master, Prorecruit). Reservation only for guaranteed-reach brand buys booked upfront.
- **IF you might change the objective later THEN don't publish until it's right** (My Online Master). Post-publish = rebuild.
- **IF Meta pushes the Advantage+ / recommended version of an objective THEN take the manual version and switch audience to Original** (Digital Growth Tutor), if you run manual controls.

**Default numbers experts use**

- ~50 purchase (or conversion) events per week per ad set to exit the learning phase (Christian Jamal).
- Traffic performance-goal options: landing page views, link clicks, daily unique reach, conversions, impressions (My Online Master).
- Awareness performance-goal options: reach, impressions, ad recall lift (Prorecruit).
- Business owners: two objectives that matter, Lead and Sales (Paul Chinedu Nnamani).

**Pre-flight checklist**

1. Name the money-goal in one sentence (a booked call? a $ purchase? a form fill?).
2. Does the purchase complete online with no human follow-up? Yes = Sales, No = Leads.
3. Objective set correctly on screen one (it locks at publish).
4. Buying type = Auction.
5. Optimisation/performance goal = the money event, not a cheaper proxy.
6. For Sales: pixel Purchase event confirmed firing. For Leads: LP form or instant form path chosen.
7. Audience switched to Original (manual control), placements can stay Advantage+.

**Top 5 failure modes**

1. **Wrong objective for the goal** (Traffic when you wanted sales). Fix: run the funnel test, rebuild on the right objective.
2. **Sales objective with an offline close.** Meta never sees the purchase, delivery starves. Fix: switch to Leads (Christian Jamal).
3. **Right objective, proxy optimisation goal** ("maximize link clicks" under Leads turns it back into Traffic). Fix: optimise to the lead/purchase event (Christian Jamal).
4. **Publishing before the objective is final**, then discovering you can't change it. Fix: finalise pre-publish or rebuild (My Online Master).
5. **Accepting Advantage+ Audience because "targeting no longer matters."** Fix: switch to Original Audience per our manual doctrine, base overrides the source framing.

## Applied to your business

**Which of your offers this feeds:** list the offers you actually put cold paid traffic behind, with real prices, e.g. `<your offline-closed offer + price>` (sold on a call or in the room), `<your self-serve online offer + price>` (paid for on your site with no human step), and any offer you are not currently advertising. Objective choice is the first decision on each. Note the account they run on: `<YOUR_AD_ACCOUNT_ID>`, Page `<YOUR_PAGE_ID>`, pixel `<YOUR_PIXEL_ID>`.

**Consensus translated into moves:**

1. **Offers that close offline go to the Leads objective.** If the buyer fills a form or books, and a human closes them live or on a call rather than an online checkout, that is a textbook Leads case (Christian Jamal's offline-close rule). Optimise to the lead event, never link clicks, and write `<your target CPL>` down before launch. One counting trap worth knowing: read lead counts from the specific lead action type, never from a summed `actions` total, which double-counts and will make your CPL look better than it is.
2. **Offers that complete as a real online purchase go to the Sales objective, optimising to Purchase.** No human follow-up means Meta can see the money, exactly Christian Jamal's Sales case. Before you pick this, confirm the Purchase event actually fires and reaches Meta cleanly; if it does not, optimise to a mid-funnel event until the volume and the signal exist.
3. **Leave buying type on Auction, always.** Reservation is not for direct response.
4. **Decide once whether you run manual audiences or Advantage+ Audiences, and be consistent.** If you have chosen manual, switch to Original Audience when Meta nudges you. Note the manual/Advantage+ flow was consolidated in early 2026, so re-verify what the live UI actually offers before assuming the toggle is where this brain says.
5. **Guard the optimisation goal.** Optimise to the lead or Purchase event, never to link clicks or landing-page views, or the campaign quietly becomes a traffic campaign (Christian Jamal).

**What does NOT apply to most small service businesses:**

- **App Promotion:** no app, ignore the objective entirely.
- **The Sales objective on an offline-closed offer:** tempting but wrong. If the sale closes off-platform, Meta starves on missing purchase data. That offer stays on Leads.
- **The e-commerce catalog / Advantage+ Shopping path** some sources tie to Sales: a manual campaign optimising to the pixel Purchase event is the fit, not a catalog build.
- **Reservation buying type:** brand and guaranteed-reach use case, irrelevant to lead-gen economics.
