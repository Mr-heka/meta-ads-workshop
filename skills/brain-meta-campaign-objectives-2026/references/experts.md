# Experts Mined: Meta campaign objectives 2026 (current objective set, buying types, optimisation goals)

Per creator: name, popular or hidden-gem (from the source index built at mining time), angle, and what they uniquely add. Ordered by usefulness to this survey brain.

## Prorecruit Learning Society, hidden gem
- **Angle:** shortest, most literal survey of the six-objective set, walked live from the Ads Manager create screen (April 2026).
- **Uniquely adds:** the cleanest single recital of all six objectives by name plus a one-line purpose each, and the clearest on-screen confirmation that buying type defaults to auction. Best "here are the six and what each is for" source. Thin on reservation and Andromeda.

## Christian Jamal, hidden gem
- **Angle:** practitioner/agency framework built around one decision, Leads vs Sales for service businesses closing offline.
- **Uniquely adds:** the sharpest Sales-vs-Leads rule in the set (50 purchase events/week to exit learning; Sales only when the whole purchase is online), the four-bucket model (objective / performance goal / tracking / optimization event) that scopes objective choice as step one, and a real revenue proof ($442K by month two). The single most valuable source for your funnel decision.

## Paul Chinedu Nnamani, hidden gem
- **Angle:** direct-response practitioner narrowing six objectives to a hard rule for business owners.
- **Uniquely adds:** the "business owners only ever run Lead or Sales" heuristic, treating Awareness/Traffic/Engagement/App Promotion as niche (bloggers, big brands, app companies). Also the no-pixel path (WhatsApp + Lead instant form need no pixel) and the behaviour-segment list. Reinforces the funnel framing.

## My Online Master, popular (strong engagement for size)
- **Angle:** two hands-on step-by-step walkthroughs (a Sales campaign, and a full post-Andromeda setup Part 8), best UI-level match to the brain scope.
- **Uniquely adds:** the explicit auction buying-type definition, the objective-locked-at-publish rule, the full Traffic performance-goal list with the link-click vs landing-page-view distinction, and the on-screen Andromeda audience-override note (with the manual Original-Audience opt-out we also use). The reference for what objective + buying type + optimisation goal look like in the current UI.

## V.S.L Digital Marketing Hub, hidden gem
- **Angle:** beginner masterclass anchoring the six-objective concept in a non-Facebook analogy (Indomi noodles) before mapping the mismatch mistake.
- **Uniquely adds:** the crispest "objective = the goal you want the ad to achieve" definition and the clearest statement of the objective/goal mismatch failure. Pure orientation, no platform-mechanics depth.

## Edunexia Digital Marketing, hidden gem
- **Angle:** structured course-lesson delivery (translated/localised), most textbook one-line definition of each objective plus a basic pixel tie-in.
- **Uniquely adds:** the "Meta pre-divides users into categories" framing in plain terms and the Sales-objective-needs-pixel point. A thin, plain baseline reference (17 views), current 6-objective set with no stale UI.

## Digital Growth Tutor, popular (strong engagement for size)
- **Angle:** pure end-to-end UI walkthrough of one objective (Traffic) for beginners who want manual control over Advantage+.
- **Uniquely adds:** the manual-over-Advantage+ choice at every level (Traffic campaign, audience switched to Original, placements left on), the Traffic conversion-location list (Website/App/Messenger/Instagram/Facebook/Calls), and the landing-page-views-over-clicks optimisation rationale. Aligns tightly with our manual doctrine.

## HubSpot Marketing (Ross Simmons), popular, ERA-FLAGGED
- **Angle:** agency framing of the Advantage+ era as removing the manual/Advantage+ toggle, replaced by setup-criteria-triggered automation (July 2025).
- **Uniquely adds:** context on the Andromeda buying-type shift and the "optimise for purchase not add-to-cart/view-content" point. Used only as caveated background for Theme 9, never as a primary backer. Its toggle/placement/WhatsApp claims are a mid-2025 transition snapshot, not settled 2026 state.

## Source quality notes

- **Usable on-scope sources: 8 of 11.** Prorecruit, Christian Jamal, Paul Chinedu Nnamani, My Online Master (x2 videos, one creator), V.S.L, Edunexia, Digital Growth Tutor. HubSpot counts as a caveated 8th (context only).
- **Excluded, 2:** Eric | Ecom Explained (thin, off-scope, pure campaign structure/budget, never names the objectives; belongs to `brain-meta-budgets-bidding-learning`). Damini Tripathi (off-scope, learning-phase/creative/CAPI tactics; belongs to `brain-meta-budgets-bidding-learning` + `brain-meta-pixel-capi-signals`).
- **Freshness:** all sources pass the <=12-month rule. One exception to treat with care: HubSpot Marketing is July 2025 and frames its claims as an in-progress "2025" shift, so its specific toggle/placement behaviour is a transition point, not the final 2026 state.
- **Transcription artifact:** both My Online Master videos and the second creator repeatedly transcribe "Andromeda" as "Android." This is a mishearing, not a claim about the Android OS.
- **Thin sub-areas of the locked scope (stated plainly, not padded):**
  - **Reservation buying type**, no source covers it beyond confirming auction is the default. What we know: reservation is the second buying type, for guaranteed reach/frequency brand buys booked upfront, not for direct-response. That is the ceiling of the evidence.
  - **App Promotion objective**, named in every list but never explained beyond "app installs / app events." Not relevant if you have no app, so the thinness costs us nothing.
  - **Optimisation-event depth inside an objective**, deliberately shallow here by scope; the deep treatment is `brain-meta-optimisation-event-strategy`.
- **Source mix skew:** the strongest sources are hidden-gem small channels selected on engagement, not views. Six of eight usable sources are ODAX/objective explainers rather than auction-theory deep dives, so the survey is well-covered but the auction internals lean on the wider verified evidence rather than these transcripts.
