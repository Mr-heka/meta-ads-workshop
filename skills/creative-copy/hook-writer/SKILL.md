# Hook Writer

The cover slide does one job: earn the swipe. It does not explain, summarise, or
sell. Every hook this skill writes is judged on one question only: does someone
mid-scroll stop for it.

If `~/.claude/brand-kit.md` exists, read it first and obey its voice, audience
and banned-words rules. If it does not exist, suggest running /brand-kit once,
then continue without it.

## What to ask for (once, then work)

You need two things before writing anything:

1. The single idea the carousel delivers. One sentence. If the user gives a
   topic ("productivity"), push once for the specific claim ("you lose 2 hours a
   day to app switching").
2. Who it is for. One line. A hook for founders is not a hook for designers.

Never ask more than these two. If the user pastes a full carousel, extract both
yourself and confirm in one line.

## The 10 formulas

Write exactly one hook per formula, in this order:

1. **Number + tension** — "5 skills you already pay for and never switched on"
2. **Negative warning** — "Stop designing carousels slide by slide"
3. **Us vs the default** — "Everyone writes the cover first. Do it last."
4. **Result, no method** — "This carousel took 4 minutes to design"
5. **Call out the reader** — "If your last post died at 12 likes, read this"
6. **Open loop** — "The third slide is the one nobody writes"
7. **Before/after** — "12 likes to 4,000 saves. Same content, new covers."
8. **Contrarian claim** — "Your content is fine. Your covers are the problem."
9. **Cost of inaction** — "Every weak cover costs you the whole carousel"
10. **Simple promise** — "Design a week of carousels in one sitting"

Swap the example content for the user's idea. Keep every hook under 12 words;
under 8 is better. No colons doing the work a claim should do.

## Rules that hold on every hook

- The hook must be true. A number in a hook must exist in the carousel.
- One idea per hook. If it needs a comma to breathe, cut it in half.
- No engagement-bait ("you won't believe", "wait for it").
- No hashtags, no emoji on the cover unless the brand kit says otherwise.
- Write for the reader's problem, not the author's topic.

## Output format

A numbered list of the 10 hooks, each tagged with its formula name, then your
top 3 ranked with one line each on why, judged as: specificity, tension,
brevity. Finish by asking which one to hand to /slide-builder.
