# Playbook — Meta Ads Creative Strategy (creative is the targeting)

Lens: small-business lead-gen with a locked brand system. Doctrine: the ad defines the audience; leverage = research + hook + angle + format. Run finished work through your own brand and claims rules. The hook banks are inline below.

---

## 1. Creative IS the targeting

Strongest consensus in the corpus. Interest stacks + lookalikes dead as primary levers; Meta finds the buyer, the ad decides who gets found.

- **Theriot:** "It's the ad level... each ad has its own audience it targets." "Facebook pulls hundreds and thousands of data points from that ad... 80% lies in the creative." "My creative creates the audience." "You pick the audience by the bait, which is the ad itself." "Audience targeting is dead weight." No interests/LAL since Dec 2022.
- **Hunyor:** "The targeting now happens on the creative side." **Denney:** "It's ultimately the creative that drives the targeting." **Fedotoff:** "Stop trying to outgamble Facebook algorithm... smarter than all of us combined" — match model age/look to buyer age.
- **Blue Sense evidence:** ~30% of any interest group is mis-assigned (interaction, not intent). Leverage argument: 5% from bid tweaks vs "double or triple the account" from better creative.
- **Hormozi (same principle, organic):** "The content is the targeting. If you want the right avatar, make content for that avatar."
- **Small-account caveat (contested):** Chappell — under $30k/mo you're still training the model, single-interest ad sets still work. Piliero — day 1: 1 broad + 2 niche interests to steer toward broad.

## 2. Hooks & thumbstop (first 3 seconds)

- **Numbers:** hook rate = 3-sec plays ÷ impressions; >10% good, <5% poor (Heath); Hunyor ≥25% ecom benchmark. Heath's proof it's not a decider: 16% hook-rate ad lost to a 10% one on cost-per-purchase.
- **Hott:** judge the literal first frame ("Second 0") — "enough to stop the scroll for the RIGHT person, not any person." Weak first frame throttles distribution: "Meta knows very rapidly... it's not going to waste money spending on it." SETTING is the most under-weighted relevance signal: "What's in the background matters so much... it beams relevance into the consumer's eyes" — brief multiple settings. Hook in the customer's words: past-tense first-person, 3rd-5th grade. 2025-26: users on "high alert to anything that doesn't matter to them" → "Blend in to stand out." Opening frame product-first or problem-first, never logo/title card; 9:16, bold native text.
- **Hott "live in the problem":** 15-17s of problem-immersion before product can beat product-in-second-one; "stack five problems in 15 seconds."
- **Theriot 3-second visual-hook test:** same video/messaging ×3, first 3 seconds get a different *visual* hook, text hook held constant.
- **Theriot 3 Essential Hook Elements:** (1) understood instantly with zero context by least-interested prospect; (2) call out an existing want, then the named mechanism; (3) instant clarity + curiosity. "Great hooks create instant clarity and curiosity." He has 13 hook-formula categories keyed to awareness.
- **Hormozi:** "Once you've written your headline, you've spent 80 cents of your advertising dollar" — a great hook beats control 2-5x. Curiosity is king, different is ideal; "all ads have headlines, just bad ones and good ones." Call out who it's for/not-for. "You advertise to one person." Show the moment, don't tell. "Fancy equals friction, simple equals sales." Steal like an artist.

**Hook library (attributed):**
- Theriot by awareness — Unaware: "I go to work in tears everyday because I hate my job." · Problem: "I'm broke...what should I do?" · Solution: "Laziest way to make money online for beginners." · Product: "The only forex trading strategy you will ever need." · Most: "Forex Trading Mastery now 50% off."
- Theriot angle-discovery case ($150k→$2.5M/mo): "all we did was find two girls that tagged each other and said, 'Girls night soon?'"
- Hormozi: "Let People Believe You Got Lucky." / "Meetings are the biggest time waster in any company."

## 3. Angles & messaging

**5 Stages of Market Awareness (Theriot, credited 60M+ client revenue)** — coldest/largest → warmest/smallest; the stage decides what the ad may say:
1. Unaware — speak to mindset (no problem/product/desire named).
2. Problem Aware — speak to the problem.
3. Solution Aware — speak to the desire/outcome.
4. Product Aware — prove superiority, build trust.
5. Most Aware — discount/offer.
"90% of marketers only run Stage 5 ads" → smallest pond, can't scale. Build across stages to keep pulling colder audiences.

**Generating angles (research, not guessing):**
- Theriot's 3 sources + Reddit: Meta Ad Library (ads running 6-12 months = proven; model the principle), internal assets (comments/reviews/DMs), **2- and 3-star reviews** (real pain/objections in plain language), Reddit phrase-mining → personas + a bank of angles. "The fastest way to scale isn't random testing, it's a repeatable, research-driven creative system."
- Hott Empathy-First: "Think how they think. Speak how they speak. Consume what they consume. The ads will write themselves." Concept = core idea + format; angle = which problem/benefit/audience. "The best ideas are already hiding in your ad account."
- General 8 angles: Pain / Outcome / Social proof / Curiosity / Comparison / Urgency / Identity / Contrarian. Lead-gen 9: Build day / Bottleneck / Proof / Authority / Event urgency / Contrarian / Lead magnet / Comparison / Founder-led.

**Framing — Theriot Emotion Baiting:** "You can't create desire, only tap what exists" → identify your process-to-result → name the mechanism (curiosity) → lead with emotional outcome, reveal product second → "SHOW what people want, don't just say it... that fires dopamine" → first-person emotional copy.

**How many angles:** Hott 6-10 ugly concepts across angles, "volume and diversity over polish"; ad-creative 3-5 distinct angles before writing headlines.

**Scaling on psychology (Theriot triangle):** Desire (bigger/universal), Sophistication (evolve claims as market tires: plain → bigger → new mechanism → identity), Awareness (open colder stages).

## 4. Offer (Hormozi)

- Give-give-give-ask: "Give away the secrets, sell the implementation." "Your free stuff should be better than their paid stuff." 3-to-1 give:ask; advanced — pure-give content, put the ask only in ads ("it protects the brand"). "CTAs in content kill reach" (organic; the ad holds the ask).
- Lead magnet = "a complete solution to a narrowly defined problem" — the thing before the thing. Never a partial solution. Three types (reveal a problem / free sample / one step); four formats (software / info / service / physical); 7-step build; "if it isn't good enough that people tell others, you've failed."
- SPCL proof (frame value not price): Status (open with proof), Power (say-do correspondence — the most powerful), Credibility (visible third-party proof), Likeness ("just be you"). Proof is the educator's "Vegas lights"/Lamborghini. "How narrowly can I define the problem so that in that tiny pond I am king."
- **Applied example (service business):** lead with proof (real counts, time-stamped, named mechanisms), give real frameworks/prompts/steps free, build a "reveal a problem" free audit, sell the on-site rollout. Hard walls: no outcome guarantees, no refund, no support promises, no "lifetime access", process language only.

## 5. Formats

- Hott format library (each a concept type): socially-native stills · fake-UGC video · employee-generated · founder/expert talking head · problem-solution · news-style · meme-style. Problem-solution converts + reads fastest → faster usable signal.
- Theriot: never mix photos and videos in one test — "three photos OR three videos."
- Hormozi: long-form = lists/steps/stories only; "a long video is many shorts strung together"; short-form platforms absorb 5-10/day, feed platforms 1-2/day; "shorts exist to earn the long-form watch" (~480 shorts ≈ 2h long-form).
- ad-concept-miner: win by finding a winning *concept* and generating many variations within it, not spamming near-identical ads.
- **Ugly ads (Hott):** deliberately low production so it feels native. "The more ugly you make things, the more authentic it can look" — a spectrum, not a destination. "Ugly ≠ bad — you still need a good ad." ~60 min end-to-end on an iPhone + CapCut. Sniff Test (each yes = smells like an ad): heavy first-frame branding · studio lighting · blank studio background · obviously-not-a-phone · heavily scripted dialogue.
- Hunyor: no AI-made creative — "Meta ads are not at the level where they can make good ads."
- **Specs:** Meta copy — primary text ~125 visible (2,200 max), headline ~40, description ~30; front-load hook. Image ratios — 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9, 1.91:1; text overlay ≤8 words; 3 variants always. Lock your brand tokens (colours, type weight, tracking) in a render template; image generators rarely hold exact brand colour/font → render branded assets from your own template.

## 6. Direct-response copy

- Structure (Theriot): first-person, emotional, the lived experience not the spec sheet; emotional outcome first, product second. Test 2 primary texts + 2 headlines, "one new and one winner."
- Benefit>feature (ad-creative "Headlines That Click"): specific over vague ("Cut reporting time 75%" not "Save time"), benefits over features, active voice, real numbers. Avoid jargon, "Best/Leading/Top", all-caps, clickbait the LP can't deliver. Descriptions complement (proof, objections, urgency), don't repeat headlines.
- Hormozi CTA: ask lives in ads / description / link-in-bio, not organic. "Be clear not clever."
- PAS / problem-solution: Hott (converts + reads faster) and Theriot (Emotion Baiting) both lean explicit problem→solution.
- **Compliance (hard, repeated everywhere):** no outcome guarantees, no "lifetime access", no refund or money-back promises, no support or helpdesk promises, no fabricated facts, no invented statistics. Run through your own brand and claims rules. Do NOT carry practitioners' money-back/income-claim styles into your copy.

## 7. Creative testing (production side)

- Volume: Hott 6-10 concepts/round; Theriot each concept = a new DCT ad set, "~1 in 10 DCTs takes majority of spend and performs"; cadence 2-3 new DCTs / 7 days, 3-5 ad sets live (1 winners + ≤4 DCTs), each 3 photos OR 3 videos + one new idea.
- 3-2-2 (Charley T): 3 creatives × 2 headlines × 2 texts (cap 20) in a DCT. 3222 (Pawliw): + 2 landing pages ("Meta crawls and ranks your pages"). "Your guesswork will never beat the scientific method." "Spend is a meritocracy." Purpose of a test: "did this change make the campaign better or worse?" — not "find winners to abuse."
- One variable per test (Theriot): "same hook, different visual"; test real contenders not filler.
- Iterate winners: cut new hooks from existing footage, re-angle. Never touch the winner — do something next to it; duplicate via post ID (Hunyor: "never edit an existing ad... pause and duplicate"). Post-ID archaeology: old winners still win 7-8/10.
- Concept-miner loop (ad-concept-miner): pull (`ads_library_search`, tight niche phrase, ACTIVE, limit 40; discard 100k+ result counts) → teardown (format/hook/copy/visual/offer/cta/label) → concept board (cluster, rank, flag what you cannot copy) → build on-brand variations under your own brand rules. Honest limit: public Ad Library shows creative + dates, NOT spend/results; "winner" inferred from repetition/recurrence.
- Judge: Theriot wait ≥3 days then 7-day window ("great ads take off within 48 hours"); Hott ~20 conversions/ad set; a 1,000+ impression floor; small budget → hold longer, weight qualified sign-ups over thumbstop.

## 8. Creative diagnostics — diagnose, never decide

Unanimous: secondary metrics explain WHY; cost-per-lead / CPA / ROAS decide WHAT dies.
- Hott: "Higher CTR or hook rate doesn't mean an ad performs better... they can't tell you if the clicks and attention are relevant" — sometimes the lowest-CTR ad drives the most sales.
- Heath: "Don't optimize for those — use them to understand what's going on." 16% hook-rate ad lost to a 10% one.
- Diagnostic pairings (Heath): good hook + low CTR = weak offer/proof; good CTR + poor conversion = landing page; link-click vs LP-view gap >50% = slow page/junk placement. Frequency cold ≤2.5, warm 6-8, hot 10+ ok; Scalability School won't kill a good-CPA ad on frequency (variety = "seven different ads").
- Theriot's fix order when an ad won't spend (it's the creative, not the buying): showing (not saying) what people want? · hook understood instantly with no context? · differentiated ("if your ad looks like every other brand, why would Facebook favor you")? · researched angle / real desire? · mass-market desire?
- Hott "the call is coming from inside the house": "It's probably your fault, not Meta's." Audit order website (hero → LP → offer) → creative → structure. "Turning off the top spending ad is an atrocity" — it's an indicator, not the cause. Relevance chain: ad hook → LP hook → homepage hero must connect.
- Attribution: Hott real account 7DC1DV ROAS 2.18 vs incremental 1.21 (1-day-view warm conversions that'd convert anyway) — re-check any creative that wins too well warm, poorly cold.

## 9. Avoiding Meta's creative automation

OFF: Advantage+ creative enhancements (Hunyor/Blue Sense), Flexible Ads for testing (Pawliw/Blue Sense; Chappell dissents for validated variations), Advantage+ Audiences → "switch to original audience" (Theriot: frequency skyrockets, faster burnout), Audience Network (Hunyor 14%-CTR trap), manual sales not Advantage+ Shopping (Theriot), untick "use as a suggestion" on retargeting (Piliero), don't edit live ads, launch at midnight (Pawliw). KEEP ON: Advantage+ Placements (Theriot).
Endorsed exception: DCT permutation matching — Meta permutes your capped human-designed 3-2-2 grid; you own the inputs. ASC verdicts: Theriot controlled test manual 2.91 vs ASC 2.14 ROAS; Charley T "makes an A-minus account a C-plus"; Fogarty pro for simple accounts.

## 10. Contrarian / disputed (both sides)

- All-broad vs train-with-interests under $30k (Theriot/Fogarty vs Chappell/Piliero) — most relevant for a modest account.
- AI creative: against for branded (Hunyor; Nano Banana can't hold brand) vs fine for fast non-branded demos.
- Flexible ads: off for testing (Pawliw/Blue Sense) vs validated variations (Chappell).
- Do secondary metrics ever matter? No on kill/scale (Hott/Heath) — but both use them (trend indexing, diagnostic pairings) to inform the *next* creative.
- Judge speed: Theriot 48h/7-day vs Hott ~20 conversions vs ad-creative 1,000 impressions.
- CTA placement: Hormozi (out of organic, only ads) vs hard-DR (CTA in every asset).
- Ugly vs brand-locked: Hott max-ugly phone footage vs a locked brand render — reconcile: ugly for founder/UGC video, render engine for text+logo statics/carousels.
