# Experts Mined - Meta attribution truth (windows, view-through, incrementality, when ROAS lies)

15 videos mined 2026-07-06. Hidden-gem flags taken from the source index built at mining time (gems surfaced on top-decile engagement for their channel size, not raw views).

---

## Umar Tazkeer - hidden gem (3 videos in the set)
- **Angle:** straight UI-walkthrough teacher. Defines attribution mechanically inside live Ads Manager, then uses the built-in Compare Attribution Settings tool to show how small the click-vs-view and standard-vs-incremental gaps usually are for lead-gen.
- **Uniquely adds:** the exact click paths (ad-set attribution settings; Performance tab > Compare Attribution Settings > Incremental Attribution). The most on-model data point in the set: for lead-gen and D2C he sees little gap (426 vs 423 leads; 12 vs 9 purchases). Names the exact symptom this brain exists to answer ("10 purchases in Meta, 5 in our dashboard").

## Ryan Stewart - hidden gem
- **Angle:** agency owner stitching Facebook + Calendly + GoHighLevel + ActiveCampaign into a true cost-per-signed-client report with Claude. The clearest lead-gen translation of the ROAS-overstatement problem in the whole set.
- **Uniquely adds:** the CRM-scoreboard case study closest to a lead-gen model. Spent ~$40k with zero signed clients; the month with the most booked calls signed zero. Real number that matters = ~$4,700 cost per client, not cost per lead. This is our GHL reconciliation argument, made by someone using the same GHL stack.

## Caden Thompson - hidden gem
- **Angle:** practitioner debating his own prior stance in public. Was 7-day-click-only for a year, now enabling 1-day view, tying the reversal to the Andromeda full-funnel update.
- **Uniquely adds:** the honest contested view on view-through (the one Theme 3 voice arguing to turn it ON), plus the spend/LTV thresholds and the "watch for view-throughs to spike" warning. **Era/scope flag:** personal-stance reversal, tied to single-ad-set full-funnel structure; used only inside the contested theme, not as consensus.

## Jason Gan - hidden gem
- **Angle:** very short caution piece. The mistake is shrinking the window without understanding what you lose; the fix is trusting the default.
- **Uniquely adds:** the cleanest "shortening the window is a learning problem, not a reporting cosmetic" worked example (10 conversions, only 3 in day one, the other 7 are real sales the campaign goes blind to). Quotes the Ads Manager tooltip directly.

## Dara Denney - strong engagement (popular, 101k views)
- **Angle:** creative-strategist/agency lens treating Ads Manager as a two-question diagnostic (what's working, why). Pushes back on over-trusting CTR and third-party attribution tools for smaller brands.
- **Uniquely adds:** the "primary metrics vs storytelling metrics" framing (decide on four, everything else explains), the breakdown-effect explanation (Meta caps profitable-looking ads on purpose), and the ruling that most brands do not need Triple Whale/Northbeam.

## Ben Heath - strong engagement (popular)
- **Angle:** media-buyer/agency owner. This video is about the "maximise value of conversions" bid goal, not attribution windows.
- **Uniquely adds:** the reported-vs-true-value *under*-reporting case (Hyros tracked £96k, £58k unreported by Meta on recurring revenue) and the value-based lead-gen framing (feed back different lead values via different thank-you pages). **Scope flag:** not an attribution-window source; used only for the reported-vs-true-value gap.

## Sam Piliero - strong engagement (popular)
- **Angle:** interview with an actual Meta executive (Director of Signal Growth). Platform-official framing on the newest Incremental Attribution feature.
- **Uniquely adds:** the strongest standard-vs-incremental gap numbers (regular ROAS 15.77 vs incremental 2.85), Meta's own claimed "24% lift," the compatibility details (objectives, conversion locations, value optimisation), and the adopt-with-a-lift-test workflow. Rare Meta-side admission of the over-reporting scale.

## Dr. Matt Shiver - strong engagement
- **Angle:** practitioner running lead-gen and DM-heavy campaigns for service businesses (booked calls, schedules). Cross-references Facebook against Hyros as an outside source of truth. Closest analog to CRM reconciliation.
- **Uniquely adds:** the explicit lead-gen rule (turn OFF 1-day view, keep 7-day click), the real DM-campaign inflation number (>10% of booked calls from 1-day view), and the pixel double-fire failure (Facebook reported 8 booked calls, only 2 real, one unqualified). Also the low-ticket 1-day-click carve-out.

## Common Thread Collective (Taylor Holiday and team) - strong engagement (4 videos in the set)
- **Angle:** agency-side, grounded in their own year-long geo-holdout incrementality testing rather than platform marketing. Argues the click-vs-view debate is the wrong question.
- **Uniquely adds:** the rigorous incrementality-% definition (reported vs holdout, "100% incremental"), the discount-factor maths (2:1 × 0.8 = 1.6 IROAS), the "hold your setting constant to build a measurement system" discipline, the IA mechanism (continuous control-vs-treatment), and the system-design scale view (Starving Giants, 3.76x average incrementality factor). The most methodologically serious voice in the set.

## Max Rosewater - strong engagement
- **Angle:** access-driven explainer who interviewed Meta's marketing/auction engineering team. Focus on Andromeda mechanics behind IA.
- **Uniquely adds:** the "rules-based windows are proxies" line straight from Meta, the plain definition of a true incremental conversion, Meta's "46% lift / 37 studies / 30 advertisers / eight verticals" stat, and the note that IA does not yet split view vs click in its own reporting. **Era flag:** rollout was ~50% of advertisers at filming (June 2025) and the click/view breakdown was forthcoming; treat the reporting-gap detail as time-bound.

## Jamie Stenton - strong engagement
- **Angle:** practitioner UI walkthrough using a real anonymised client account to make the standard-vs-incremental ROAS gap concrete.
- **Uniquely adds:** the lemonade-stand analogy (10 baseline, 14 with the sign, only 4 incremental), a clean real-client half-the-ROAS example (4.8x standard vs 2.4x incremental), the compare-column path, and the honest "Meta hasn't disclosed the methodology, so it's just a data point" caveat. **Era flag:** his "keep standard, treat IA as a data point" stance is dated April 2025 and framed as temporary.

## David Parrottino - strong engagement
- **Angle:** ecom operator/agency voice giving a clean two-column mental model: standard = optimisation tool, incremental/blended = financial truth.
- **Uniquely adds:** the sharpest one-liner ("assumes causation, not contribution"), the "all platforms over-attribute" list (Google, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest, Klaviyo), the geo-holdout worked example (turn Meta down 50%, 40% drop = real, 5% = capturing demand), and the operator/ad-buyer split.

## Source quality notes

- **Usable sources: 13 of 15.** All 15 returned substantive notes; none were thin. Two are used with scope/era flags rather than dropped: **Caden Thompson** (personal-stance reversal, quarantined to the contested view-through theme) and **Ben Heath** (a bid-goal video, used only for the reported-vs-true-value gap, never as an attribution-window source). Both still contribute genuine, on-topic material, so they count toward the usable total; only their misuse is fenced off.
- **Above the 8-usable-source bar** with room to spare, so no padding was needed.
- **Freshness:** all sources fall inside the 18-month video window. Two carry time-bound details, flagged inline: Rosewater's ~50% IA rollout and forthcoming view/click breakdown (June 2025), and Stenton's April 2025 "treat IA as a data point" stance. IA itself dates to ~April 2025 across Piliero, Shiver, Rosewater and CTC, so the feature is current, not speculative.
- **Concentration risk:** Common Thread Collective supplies 4 of 15 videos and Umar Tazkeer 3 of 15, so 7 of 15 come from two voices. Their material is consistent and high-quality, but the incrementality-methodology and geo-holdout themes lean heavily on CTC; treat those as one well-argued school, not independent confirmation.
- **Thin sub-areas of the locked scope, stated honestly rather than padded:**
  - **Engaged-view (video) window** is barely covered. Only Shiver and Thompson mention it, both deferring the decision to "case-by-case on volume." No source gives a firm rule.
  - **The specific mechanics of the settings change over time** (what Meta added/removed and when) are only touched via the Andromeda references; no source walks a before/after of the UI.
  - **Practical incrementality and CRM reconciliation at small spend** are surveyed here but not worked in depth. That is the deliberate boundary with the sibling deep brain `brain-meta-attribution-measurement-deep` (P5), which owns the hands-on holdout and GHL-reconciliation detail.
- **Model of the market:** ecommerce/ROAS-framed sources dominate. Only Ryan Stewart and Dr. Matt Shiver are natively lead-gen/CRM. Every ecommerce claim in this brain has been translated to the lead-gen/GHL scoreboard in the synthesis and playbook.
