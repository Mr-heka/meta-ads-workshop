# Experts Mined: Meta Account Health and Policy

Per creator: name, popular or hidden-gem status (from the source index built at mining time), their angle, and what they uniquely add. Two sources excluded for era/thin flags (documented at the bottom).

---

## Ben Heath: HIDDEN GEM (small channel, top-decile engagement)
- **Angle:** High-spend agency operator (1000+ ad accounts, ~$97M/yr spend) giving a calm, procedural escalation ladder for disabled ad accounts.
- **Uniquely adds:** The clearest distinction between accidental disablement and real policy violations, the "tailor your appeal message to the truth" rule, and the strongest warning against paid recovery services and bought "seasoned" accounts. His point that prioritised support scales with spend and tenure explains why small accounts get slow appeals. Highest-authority voice in the set on recovery.

## Master The Meta: EXCLUDED (both videos)
- Two videos, both promoting an unverified third-party browser extension that auto-submits account/BM IDs to an unknown backend. One flagged thin, one flagged as a ToS/security risk. Not used. See exclusions.

## Mind Mentor: HIDDEN GEM (small channel, top-decile engagement)
- **Angle:** South Asian agency operator focused on prevention: segmenting risky ad categories onto separate IDs, portfolios, and payment methods.
- **Uniquely adds:** The named list of reliably-disabling categories (online earning, trading, betting, visa scams, medicine), the wording-matters example (a Netflix ad disabled, then ran after a slight copy change), and the payment-card insight (one card shared across ~5 ad accounts, so a flag on the card poisons all linked accounts). Best source on structural isolation of risk.

## STEFAN VAN DER MERWE: popular (82k views, strong engagement)
- **Angle:** Personal case study of a permanently disabled personal Facebook account recovered in 72 hours.
- **Uniquely adds:** The single novel recovery tactic in the set: buy Meta Verified on Instagram (a separate login surface) to unlock enhanced support with a live agent when the normal appeal flow is dead. Also the "personal account is the master key" framing and the "obstructing business operations" appeal language that moved his case. The only source on personal-profile (not ad-account) recovery.

## Kapil Rahangdale: popular (strong engagement for size)
- **Angle:** Agency/scaling operator framing account health as an infrastructure and IP-hygiene problem tied to a seasonal Q4 surge.
- **Uniquely adds:** The Q4 scrutiny-spike theory (big brands 10x their spend, Meta reallocates scrutiny), the "Limited Creative" tag symptom, domain verification as rejection prevention, warm-up spend figures, and partner-access-vs-personal-login as a trust signal. Note: his anti-detect-browser (Ads Power) segment is vendor-promotion with an affiliate code, flagged and not treated as policy.

## Christian Jamal: popular (17k + 1.2k views, two videos)
- **Angle:** Practitioner claiming $100M+ spend, giving two structured frameworks: one for disabled-account triage, one for ad-level rejection.
- **Uniquely adds:** The four-state Business Support Home diagnostic (page/account/ad account/asset restricted), the "90% cooked" severity read on personal-account bans, the "credit score for your ad account" reputation frame, the five-reason rejection diagnostic, and the "99 problem" quarantine tactic (move at-risk assets into an already-restricted portfolio). Also the philosophical spine: "Meta rewards calm, compliant, and predictable behavior." The most framework-dense creator in the set.

## Cabilly & Co.: popular (strong engagement for size)
- **Angle:** A platform-enforcement lawyer's structured escalation-ladder view, grounded in cited Meta enforcement statistics.
- **Uniquely adds:** The only legal-professional perspective. Cited numbers (134M scam ads removed, ~12M fraud accounts, 10-20% enforcement error rate). The four suspension categories, the three-tier recovery path (self / partner agency / legal letter), the "10 business days then escalate" rule, and the hard truth that "I didn't do anything wrong" does not move the needle. Best source on appeal strategy and evidence.

## Shout Marketing: popular (10k views, strong engagement)
- **Angle:** Regional (India/SEBI) walkthrough of the ad-account verification + special-ad-category flow for financial/real estate/insurance advertisers.
- **Uniquely adds:** The specific UI path for the newer special-category verification requirement and the observation that the rule caught accounts outside the named niches. The SEBI regulator field is India-specific and generalised in the synthesis, but the verify-then-select-category mechanic transfers.

## E & T Academy: popular (5k views, strong engagement)
- **Angle:** A practical, screen-recorded step-by-step appeal walkthrough for a restricted business portfolio.
- **Uniquely adds:** The literal click-path (fix Account Center name/DOB to match ID, request review, phone/email codes, upload ID), the four accepted ID document types, the ID-image-size fix (~1,500 x 1,000 pixels), and the stated 4-day turnaround vs the real sub-hour reinstatement he saw on new accounts. Best source on the identity-verification appeal mechanics.

## Godbless Iboyi: popular (small views, strong engagement)
- **Angle:** DIY media buyer framing rejection-fixing as a systematic diagnostic process (the isolation method) plus a warm-up recipe.
- **Uniquely adds:** The "isolation method" (test copy, then creative, then landing page one at a time using a safe link like google.com), the four rejection-trigger categories, the concrete warm-up sequence (aged profile, engagement ads, wait 1-2 weeks), and the landing-page fix list (privacy policy, contact info, not-Facebook-affiliated disclaimer). Cleanest diagnostic method in the set.

## Optimum Click: popular (strong engagement for size)
- **Angle:** Agency operator combining health/wellness ad-policy compliance with a hard HIPAA legal warning and a privacy-safe measurement alternative.
- **Uniquely adds:** The US-specific HIPAA pixel warning (Meta will not sign a BAA; fines $100-$50,000 per user), and the workaround stack (traffic/engagement campaigns, generic lead magnets, hashed email custom audiences, GA4 + UTMs + call tracking). Scope-flagged as US-only, but the "keep signals generic" instinct transfers.

## EduSphere: popular (very small views, strong engagement)
- **Angle:** Course-style structured policy walkthrough covering the prohibited-vs-restricted taxonomy and worked copy rewrites.
- **Uniquely adds:** The cleanest prohibited-vs-restricted taxonomy in the set, the five escalating consequences of violations, the employment special-category targeting rules, and three worked cosmetic-copy rewrites (sell the outcome, not the insecurity). Best structured reference on the policy taxonomy itself.

## Sagapixel: popular (small views, strong engagement)
- **Angle:** Healthcare-marketing agency owner giving a rapid-fire list of concrete disapproval triggers across Google and Meta with page-structure fixes.
- **Uniquely adds:** LegitScript certification, the "Botox on the page triggers disapproval" false-positive, the before/after nudity flags, and the sharpest structural rule in the set: "speculative treatment" disapproval when a landing page links out to unrelated treatments, fixed with dedicated single-treatment pages that do not link out.

## Source quality notes

- **Usable sources: 13** of 15 mined. This is above the 8-source floor, so no thinness warning is required, but the caveats below apply.
- **Excluded (2):** Both Master The Meta videos. Same pattern: an unverified third-party browser extension that auto-fills and submits appeals using your real account and BM IDs to an unknown backend. This is the exact recovery-scam pattern Ben Heath and Cabilly & Co. warn against, so including it would contradict the consensus. The finding (never use auto-submit extensions) is preserved in the synthesis exclusions.
- **Freshness:** All 15 sources passed the <=12-month rule at discovery. No stale sources.
- **Regional skew:** Several strong sources (Mind Mentor, Kapil, Shout Marketing, E & T Academy) are South Asian operators using rupee figures, SEBI, GST, and voter-ID as an ID type. The mechanics transfer; the specific regulators/currencies do not. Generalised in the synthesis and SKILL.md.
- **Vendor promotion (1 segment):** Kapil's anti-detect-browser (Ads Power) section carries an affiliate code. Flagged and excluded from the policy findings; his non-promotional prevention points are used.
- **Thin sub-areas of the scope, stated plainly:**
  - **AU-specific policy** is thin. No source gives AU-specific platform-policy detail; the SEBI/India verification flow is the closest analogue and is generalised. AU consumer-law depth is explicitly out of scope for this brain.
  - **Special ad categories** are covered mostly at the naming/taxonomy level (EduSphere, Christian Jamal, Shout Marketing). The housing/employment/credit non-discrimination targeting rules are named but not deeply worked beyond employment (EduSphere). Treat the special-category detail as directional, verify against Meta's live policy before running a financial/property/employment campaign.
  - **Financial special category** has one dedicated source (Shout Marketing) and it is India-framed. Watch for a matching AU verification prompt rather than assuming the SEBI flow.
