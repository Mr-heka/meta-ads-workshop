## How each bid strategy actually works

> "Many media buyers confuse cost caps and beat caps and treat cost caps as a lighter version less strict version of the beat caps. But it's not." - Andrei Lunev, *Bid Cap vs Cost Cap — The One Difference That Changes Everything*

> "Beat caps is the bidding strategy when the bid is frozen. It's fixed. The budget utilization for this campaign is not a priority." - Andrei Lunev, *Bid Cap vs Cost Cap — The One Difference That Changes Everything*

> "cost caps is a semi-automatic bidding campaign... it's closer to highest volume campaign to the automatic bidding. Why? Because the bid is not fixed." - Andrei Lunev, *Bid Cap vs Cost Cap — The One Difference That Changes Everything*

> "a bid cap will it'll only bid up to what you set your bid cap at. So, if you set your bid cap at $50, it will not bid for conversions that it thinks it can get for $51" - David Parrottino, *Meta Ads Strategy: Cost Cap vs Bid Cap vs Target ROAS?*

> "if we run on bid caps, we're telling Facebook, okay, like I want you to achieve this target... and then Facebook says, okay, hm, how can I ensure this is definitely the case? Who will convert most likely?" - William Kast, *Facebook Ads Bid Caps VS Highest Volume (& Which You Should Use)*

## Setting and tuning the cap

> "if you're currently getting cost per purchase of 70 in the ad account, you cannot expect cost cap to get 50 and expect it to spend your budget fully" - Marin Istvanic, *Why I Love Facebook Ads Cost Caps for Scaling, and Hate for Testing*

> "if you're getting a $150 CPA on a $100 cost cap, your actual performance is 1.5x or 1 and a halfx higher than your target. So you need to simply set your cost cap at 2/3 of what you actually want" - Cody Wittick, *5 Manual Bidding Mistakes That Are Destroying Your Ad Results (And How to Fix Them)*

> "Your cost cap will equal your average order value multiplied by your target margin percentage minus your break even cost per purchase." - Build Your Ecomm, *How To Use Cost Caps To Scale The RIGHT Way 2026*

> "don't go increasing your caps and budget every couple of hours. Give it at least 24 hours before you make increments in your budget and your caps" - Build Your Ecomm, *How To Use Cost Caps To Scale The RIGHT Way 2026*
