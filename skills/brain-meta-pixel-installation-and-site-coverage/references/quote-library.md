## The platform-vs-Meta mismatch symptom (coverage gap)

> "Imagine your Shopify dashboard says there were 100 sales today. But Meta Ads Manager shows only seven." - Big Short Ads, *Meta Pixel Doesn't Work Anymore | What to Do Instead*

> "Apple's Intelligent Tracking Prevention in Safari has reduced the expiration date of first-party cookies to just 24 hours in many cases." - Big Short Ads, *Meta Pixel Doesn't Work Anymore | What to Do Instead*
>
> **Factual correction.** The general ITP cap on script-writable first-party cookies is **7 days**, not 24 hours. The 24-hour cap is the narrower case: cookies set on a page reached via a navigation Safari classified as cross-site tracking. Plan on 7 days; treat 24 hours as the ad-click worst case.
