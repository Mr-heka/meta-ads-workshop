# Fictional prompting exercises

These are newly authored drafts, not quotations, tested clips or provider presets.
Select supported settings and attach real references separately. Durations below
are creative intentions to check against the chosen operation.

## Animate an existing product frame

Brief: the supplied frame shows a fictional ceramic mug on a wooden table.

> Keep the mug's shape, handle orientation and glaze pattern recognizable. Slowly
> move the camera from a front three-quarter view toward its side as faint steam
> rises. The mug stays on the table. Soft window light remains consistent. Quiet room
> ambience; no dialogue or music.

Inspect: handle and pattern drift, sliding, camera direction, unwanted narration.
An image-first route fits this supplied reference; it is not a universal requirement.

## Establish a scene from text

> A fictional cyclist pauses beside a riverside bench at dawn, places one foot on
> the ground and looks toward the water. Medium-wide side view, camera still. Pale
> morning light and light mist above the river. Distant birds and the bicycle chain
> settling; no speech.

Inspect: plausible contact with the ground, clear action, camera stability and sound.
No reference tag is invented because none has been attached.

## Plan two linked shots

Creative target: a short sequence whose final configured durations fit both beats.

> Shot1: Close view of a gloved hand turning a greenhouse latch. The door opens inward;
> the hinge creak begins with the movement.
>
> Shot2: From inside, a medium-wide view reveals a gardener carrying a tray of seedlings
> through that doorway. Keep the door and glove consistent. The camera gently moves
> back to make room. Footsteps and the tray settling on a bench; no music.

Use supported multishot controls or generate separate coverage for editing. Inspect
continuity and the actual cut; text labels do not guarantee an internal edit.

## Revise an observed failure

Observed result: the mug rotates instead of the camera moving. First confirm which
motion the actual settings requested. A targeted revision could read:

> The mug remains fixed in place. Only the camera moves around the mug, revealing
> the handle's side profile. Keep the tabletop grain fixed relative to the mug.

Inspect the new result before claiming the diagnosis was correct. This revision
changes one hypothesis; it does not promise another paid attempt will succeed.

## Preserve exact dialogue

> Two fictional adult colleagues stand by a workbench. Nia asks, “Ready?” Sam checks
> the closed case and replies, “Ready.” Hold a static two-shot so both speakers remain
> visible. Natural pauses, a quiet room and the latch click. No score.

Read the dialogue aloud for timing and inspect speaker assignment and lip movement.
Do not shorten a user's supplied dialogue silently to fit a duration.
