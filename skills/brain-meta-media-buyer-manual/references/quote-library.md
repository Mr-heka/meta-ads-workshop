# Quote Library — Manual Media Buying

Verbatim, attributed. Pulled from the practitioner skills + the shared `brain-meta-ads-manual-control-no-advantage` transcripts.

## On the machine vs the operator
> "You're not smarter than Facebook... don't use 2013 Google tactics on a Facebook platform and then complain when it's not working out." — Charley Tichenor
> "Andromeda is really smart. And it only stays smart if we don't spend all of our time and energy keeping it stupid." — Charley Tichenor
> "For some reason, us marketers think we're smarter than the machine." / "Meta is going to humble you often." — Spencer Pawliw
> "Facebook's data pool is way deeper than yours." — Ac Hampton
> "Meta's optimization systems and how the campaign delivers is completely literal." / "Don't helicopter parent your ad campaigns." — Ben Heath

## On ASC / Advantage+ (why manual)
> "Facebook is basically trying to get us to be their guinea pigs with no ad credits to test their specific thing." — Nick Theriot
> "It will make a D-minus account a C-plus; if you're great at Facebook it'll make your A-minus account a C-plus." — Charley Tichenor
> "All of that is out of the window and this Advantage Plus setting is basically all you need." — Davie Fogarty (the pro pole)

## On structure & consolidation
> "Meta has had concerns about audience fragmentation... they would rather you consolidated your ad sets down into just one ad set... advice that I have ignored for a long time." — Ben Heath
> "Do not have more than 1 Campaign that is built to accomplish the same business objective." — Charley Tichenor
> "The Ad is a Sales Pitch, the Ad Set is a Salesman, the Campaign is the Manager." — Charley Tichenor
> "There is no ad account structure that will save your bad ads." — Spencer Pawliw
> "The scale campaign is not special... unless you put the right ads in the scale campaign." — Sam Piliero

## On ABO vs CBO
> "If your hit rate of the tests is high enough you can squeeze more with [ABO]. If your hit rate is bad, then stick with CBO." — Scalability School
> "Spend is a meritocracy." — Charley Tichenor
> "Don't do CBOs just because everyone does it... do what works specifically for you, for your account." — Alex Fedotoff

## On testing
> "We are not testing to find winners to scale and abuse. We are testing to answer one simple question: did this change make the campaign better or worse?" — Charley Tichenor
> "No matter how good you are, your guesswork will never beat the scientific method." — Charley Tichenor
> "Each test should contain only a single variable." — Nick Theriot

## On kill / scale / never touching winners
> "Number one rule of meta media buying is never touch something that's working... Do something next to it. Don't ruin the thing that's working." — Blue Sense Digital
> "Never edit an existing ad... pause it and then instead make a duplicate." — Máté Hunyor
> "People just forget that they had a winner... a year ago. That winner is still a winner." — Scalability School
> "Slow is fast and fast is slow." — Nick Theriot
> "Every time I've seen a great ad it takes off within 48 hours." — Nick Theriot
> "I don't like cost cap for testing, but I really really love them for scaling. It's like a gladiators arena." — Scalability School

## On the math
> "It's absolutely based on conversion volume, not time." — Ben Heath (the learning phase)
> "Much better to generate a 4x return... spending a million dollars a month than a 10x return... spending 10,000 dollars a month." — Ben Heath
> "ROAS is a lie to begin with." — Charley Tichenor
> "Old school Facebook would want you to only run the 10x... New school Andromeda Facebook wants you to focus on all of these concepts." — Sam Piliero
