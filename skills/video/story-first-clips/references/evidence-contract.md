# Evidence contract

Each source record includes stable ID, original filename, location with evidence, duration and gaps, original/proxy distinction, archive pointer, residency and transcript identity. Record intervals and status for transcript review, visual inspection and perceptual listening independently.

Each candidate includes:
- Candidate ID, source ID and city; full context range.
- Viewer problem and useful lesson; why the story earns selection.
- Exact hook words and start/end times, original meaning and reorder justification.
- Ordered body ranges, their source words and expected output positions.
- Setup and payoff; any missing qualification or unresolved factual issue.
- Explicit filler/pause removals and reasons; no blanket automatic deletion.
- Duplicate group and keep/hold/reject rationale.
- Crop/profile identity and new-range visual/privacy evidence.
- Export path, version, source-to-output map, duration and hash when rendered.
- Checks actually performed and remaining review limitations.

Times must be finite, within the verified source, and ordered within each segment. Output intervals must be contiguous. Repeated source intervals need an explicit reason. A cold open must be speech, not a silence interval, and match the requested length. The conform must reject missing media, mismatched provenance and unreviewed privacy dependencies rather than silently guessing. A clearly marked unapproved first canary may be rendered after source/mask inspection to assess the treatment; its existence does not clear privacy or authorise batch reuse.

## Behavior checks when changing this skill

Use a whole source transcript with surrounding context, not a preselected answer. Check that selection finds a complete lesson without relying on scores; records rejected coverage; and separates transcript inference from visual/audio evidence.

Across genuine or explicitly synthetic behavior fixtures, include a quoted objection, a conditional claim, contradictory figures, an incomplete answer and two overlapping takes. Record absent categories as untested; do not claim one source exercises everything. A successful editorial pass preserves attribution and qualifications, holds contradictions instead of inventing corrections, and explains duplicate disposition. It may reject all candidates if the source has no worthwhile complete story.

For a real output, compare the opening against its original context, inspect frames before/after every join, decode the entire file, check audio level/peaks and listen when supported. Record a missing perceptual check as missing, not passed.
