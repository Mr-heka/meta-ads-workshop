# Experts Mined: Meta lookalike audiences and seeds

## Andrew Southworth (hidden gem)
Music-ads practitioner, small channel, top-decile engagement. Angle: literal UI mechanics
and percentage maths with an honest "sometimes works, sometimes doesn't" frame.
Uniquely adds: the 278M/2.78M rank-slice explanation, the old 100-per-country minimum
gotcha (Meta will not say which country fails), the accuracy-vs-volume seed trade
(100K loose viewers over 1K tight viewers), and cheap $5 test-and-kill discipline.

## Paul Chinedu Nnamani (hidden gem; 2 videos in the set)
Nigeria-focused targeting generalist, small channel, top-decile engagement. Angle:
lookalikes as one quarter of a four-part targeting framework, plus a dedicated
customer-list walkthrough. Uniquely adds: one-tier-per-ad-set testing discipline with
real audience sizes per band (0-1% = 555K, 5-10% = 2.6M), form-opener vs form-submitter
seed splits, the Business Manager requirement, and the one pro-leads-seed case study in
the set (15,000 CRM leads as seed, strong reported results).

## EfesAdLab (hidden gem)
Beginner-course structure creator, tiny channel, highest engagement ratio in the batch.
Angle: names the "lookalikes are dead" debate head-on and refuses a verdict. Uniquely
adds: the pre/post iOS 14 reversal (smaller used to win, broader now wins), the
"we tested it and it failed" client-facing rule, and two clean lookalike test structures
(separate campaigns, or CBO or-test).

## Blake Bauer Business (small channel, strong engagement; not gem-flagged)
Direct-response tutorial creator. Angle: 2026 platform-state walkthrough. Uniquely adds:
the removed minimum seed-size update demonstrated live (lookalike from 3 unique
customers), percentage-tier size bands, the 1/5/10 spread test, and the "further limit
the reach" hard-boundary deployment toggle.

## Christian Jamal (small channel, strong engagement; not gem-flagged)
High-spend media buyer. Angle: seed-quality filtering and the Andromeda-era reframe.
Uniquely adds: top 25% of customers by revenue as the seed, closed-deals-only for lead
gen, engagement thresholds (10+ engagements in 365 days, 75/95% views on 2-minute-plus
videos only), and the "lookalike as soft signal for Advantage+" position (which conflicts
with our verified manual-control base and is flagged as such in synthesis.md).

## Marketer Tawhida (small channel, strong engagement; not gem-flagged)
Tutorial creator. Angle: the "still worth it" framing plus granular tier guidance.
Note: her demo, numbers and key lines are near-identical to Blake Bauer Business (same
3-customer seed demo, same 1/5/10 lines), so treat the pair as corroborating one
underlying tutorial rather than two independent voices.

## SMART TECH (small channel; ERA-FLAGGED, excluded from synthesis)
Machine-translated/dubbed walkthrough, garbled phrasing throughout. Only distinct data
point is a 100-person minimum claim, kept as low-precision colour only.

## Digital Growth Tutor (small channel; ERA-FLAGGED, excluded from synthesis)
Generic evergreen step-by-step with zero engagement with Andromeda or the live debate
despite a 2026 upload. Only distinct contribution is a 1%-default recommendation,
already covered and contested by stronger sources.

## Source quality notes

- **Usable sources: 7 of 9.** That is below the 8-source comfort line; stated plainly. SMART TECH and Digital Growth Tutor were excluded from the synthesis for the reasons above.
- **Effective independent voices: closer to 6**, because Blake Bauer Business and Marketer Tawhida overlap almost completely.
- **Freshness: no exceptions.** All 9 videos passed the 12-month rule at discovery (mined 2026-07-06).
- **No big-name practitioners in this batch.** The set skews small-channel tutorial creators; the heavyweight post-Andromeda evidence (Theriot, Charley T, Piliero, Heath et al.) lives in `brain-meta-ads-manual-control-no-advantage` and was used as the cross-check base, not re-mined here.
- **Thin areas of the locked scope:** stacking tiers is covered only as "don't" (nobody in the set advocates stacking, so the pro case is unrepresented); "when broad beats lookalikes" rests mainly on EfesAdLab plus the verified base rather than fresh head-to-head tests; no source gave a controlled lookalike-vs-broad spend test with numbers. The "still worth it?" question is genuinely unsettled in the sources, and this brain reports it that way rather than manufacturing a verdict.
