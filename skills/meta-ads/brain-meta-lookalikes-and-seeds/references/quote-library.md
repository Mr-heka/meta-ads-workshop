## What a lookalike actually is

> "Here's my data. Find me people who look just like these people." - Andrew Southworth, *Will Lookalike Audiences Make Your Music Meta Ads Better?*

> "we have retargeting or custom audiences, people that have interacted with you in the past, and lookalike audiences, which are actually brand new people who have never interacted with you" - Andrew Southworth, *Will Lookalike Audiences Make Your Music Meta Ads Better?*

> "A lookalike audience is a targeted advertising segment created by algorithms to find new user who share similar characteristics, interests and behaviors with your best existing customers" - Paul Chinedu Nnamani, *The ONLY Advanced Targeting Strategies for Facebook Ads Tutorial You Need (2026)*

> "platform analyze a seed list. example top 10% of spender to identify common traits then based on those find similar people" - Paul Chinedu Nnamani, *The ONLY Advanced Targeting Strategies for Facebook Ads Tutorial You Need (2026)*

> "lookalike audience is not a warm audience. That's mistake people are making. Warm audience are people that have interacted with your business" - Paul Chinedu Nnamani, *The ONLY Advanced Targeting Strategies for Facebook Ads Tutorial You Need (2026)*

> "if there are 278 million people available to target inside the US, a 1% lookalike means the most similar 2.78 million people to your source custom audience" - EfesAdLab, *Facebook Audiences - Custom, Lookalike & Saved (Ep. 3) | Meta Ads for Beginners 2026*

## Seed quality rules everything

> "if the source sucks, the lookalike sucks. Or if the source is not relevant to the campaign you're running, the look alike is not going to be relevant" - Andrew Southworth, *Will Lookalike Audiences Make Your Music Meta Ads Better?*

> "A look-alike audience is only as good as the source you build it from" - Blake Bauer Business, *Facebook Ads Lookalike Audience Tutorial 2026 (Still Worth It?)*

> "A look-alike audience is only as good as the source you build it from. Feed the system garbage data, and you're going to get garbage audience" - Christian Jamal, *How to Create a Lookalike Audience on Meta in 2026 (The Right Way)*

> "as your custom audiences get bigger and better over time, your lookalike audiences are going to get more and more useful because more high quality data means better algorithmic models" - Andrew Southworth, *Will Lookalike Audiences Make Your Music Meta Ads Better?*

> "there's a massive difference between someone who fills out a form and someone who pulls out their credit card to pay you. You want Meta to find you more of the second group" - Christian Jamal, *How to Create a Lookalike Audience on Meta in 2026 (The Right Way)*

> "100,000 people might have opened the form, but only 10,000 people actually feed those form and submitted it" - Paul Chinedu Nnamani, *The ONLY Advanced Targeting Strategies for Facebook Ads Tutorial You Need (2026)*

## Percentage tiers and the broad reversal

> "A 1% lookalike consists on people most similar to your lookalike audience source. Increasing the percentage create a bigger broader audiences" - Paul Chinedu Nnamani, *The ONLY Advanced Targeting Strategies for Facebook Ads Tutorial You Need (2026)*

> "I would recommend if you don't want to test all them at once, create a 1%, create a 5%, and create a 10%. So that's going to give you the biggest spread" - Blake Bauer Business, *Facebook Ads Lookalike Audience Tutorial 2026 (Still Worth It?)*

> "If you don't want to test all them at once, create a 1%, create a 5%, and create a 10%. So that's going to give you the biggest spread." - Marketer Tawhida, *Facebook Ads Lookalike Audience Tutorial 2026 Still Worth It*

> "Creating broader is typically better. So, starting with that 10% is usually going to be your best audience" - Blake Bauer Business, *Facebook Ads Lookalike Audience Tutorial 2026 (Still Worth It?)*

> "Smaller lookalike audiences used to perform better before iOS 14, but after that the broader you go, the better they tend to perform. And that is the dream like being able to target broad" - EfesAdLab, *Facebook Audiences - Custom, Lookalike & Saved (Ep. 3) | Meta Ads for Beginners 2026*

> "Broad means no targeting by the way. No targeting other than age, gender, and location" - EfesAdLab, *Facebook Audiences - Custom, Lookalike & Saved (Ep. 3) | Meta Ads for Beginners 2026*
