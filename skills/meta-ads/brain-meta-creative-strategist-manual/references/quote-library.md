> "I believe that my creative creates the audience." / "You pick the audience by the bait, which is the ad itself." — Nick Theriot
> "Audience targeting is dead weight. Meta is smarter than you." — Nick Theriot
> "The targeting now happens on the creative side." — Máté Hunyor
> "It's ultimately the creative that drives the targeting and the best results on facebook ads." — Dara Denney
> "Could you spend the same amount of time just making better creative and double the account or triple the account? Definitely." — Blue Sense Digital
