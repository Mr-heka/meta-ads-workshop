# Experts Mined: Meta ads optimisation event strategy

Status (popular vs hidden gem) comes from the source index built at mining time (gem = small channel, top-decile engagement).

## Make webinar (Meta product marketing manager)
- **Status:** popular (official platform webinar, low views but the highest-authority source in the set)
- **Angle:** Meta's own current policy for lead advertisers, delivered with a Make.com CRM implementation demo.
- **Uniquely adds:** the four-part event-choice framework (down-funnel but not final, 1-40% stage completion, 28-day window, genuinely valuable), the measured lift figures (21% and 9.5% lower cost per quality lead), the match parameter ranking (lead ID first), and the only direct coverage of value optimisation in the leads objective.

## Caden Thompson
- **Status:** popular
- **Angle:** lag-time framework for lead gen; segments businesses into local/immediate, SaaS, and long-cycle B2B archetypes.
- **Uniquely adds:** the rule that sales-cycle length picks the event, the 15-30 events/month floor, and the pipeline-ratio back-calculation for cost per sold lead when the sale itself cannot be the event.

## Dr. Matt Shiver
- **Status:** popular
- **Angle:** GoHighLevel practitioner training Meta toward qualified leads and booked calls.
- **Uniquely adds:** conditional-logic thank-you pages so only qualified answers fire, pixel set to "none" on generic form submission, CAPI-only Schedule events past the 7-day pixel window, and the manual "send pixel" pipeline stage for mature accounts.

## Scott Henry (two videos)
- **Status:** popular
- **Angle:** click-by-click GHL-to-Meta plumbing for optimising to the Website Schedule event.
- **Uniquely adds:** the full pixel + CAPI build path, if/else qualification gates in booking workflows, the unique-calendar rule for ad traffic, all-parameters-on during CAPI setup, and the warning that conversion location cannot be re-edited after publish.

## Evan Seech
- **Status:** popular
- **Angle:** high-ticket call funnels end to end; coined "pixel conditioning" in this set.
- **Uniquely adds:** the match-rate-8-or-below trigger for switching to CAPI, the escalating CAPI trigger ladder (CRM stage, manual tag, AI lead scoring 7+), and profit-multiple spend gates (1x then 2x profit per customer) before kill/scale calls.

## Justin Lalonde
- **Status:** popular ($20M+ managed spend claimed)
- **Angle:** the standard events vs custom conversions distinction, cross-account data pooling vs single-account learning.
- **Uniquely adds:** the 50-conversions-in-7-days threshold before optimising to a custom event, the 200-calls/month rule for call businesses, the staged standard-then-custom path, and "in doubt, use standard events".

## Localnichemarketing

- **Uniquely adds:** the "pixel becomes what you train it on" framing, hard disqualifiers early in survey flows, the duplicate-event audit (two page-view fires per view), and custom conversions as a tracking repair tool.

## Michael Diaz

## Source quality notes

- **Usable sources: 10 of 15 discovered.** 14 transcripts mined; 1 source (Robert Dulski) had no transcript; 2 thin for scope (Ben Heath, Jared Robinson); 2 era-flagged (Tushar Dey, Rihards Zeiča) and excluded from theme backing.
- **Freshness:** all sources pass the under-12-months rule. Two era flags are content-level, not date-level: Dey presents pre-Andromeda opinion as post-Andromeda fact; Zeiča restates the long-standing 50/week rule that Meta's newer 1-40% / 28-day framework partially supersedes.
- **Thin areas, stated plainly:** value optimisation rests on ONE source (the Meta webinar via Make) and the feature was described as beta rolling to GA; treat every value-optimisation claim as single-voice. E-commerce purchase-event nuance beyond the basic default is thin (the two e-comm-specific sources are the era-flagged Dey and the entry-level Nexal). No source covers optimisation events for low-ticket digital product purchases specifically.
- **Duplication risk noted:** Scott Henry contributes two videos with overlapping setup content; his backing counts as one voice with two artefacts, not two independent voices.
