## Understand the system without inventing its rules

Meta describes Andromeda as ad retrieval before subsequent ranking. That supports distinguishing retrieval from the complete delivery system. It does not establish that targeting controls are irrelevant, that similar ads literally become one auction entry, or that an account must run a particular number of creatives. Treat those stronger statements in the inherited collection as claims requiring separate evidence.

Assess automation by function: audience expansion, placement allocation, budget allocation and creative transformations can have different trade-offs. Keep current approved account policy and the user's actual objective in view. An inherited manual-first or automation-first preference does not decide every future campaign.

## Structure around genuine constraints

Heath, Stenton, Eisenkirchner and others discuss consolidation to avoid unnecessarily thin observations. Group work when objective, economics, audience constraints and measurement allow it. Keep separate units where those requirements differ. Estimate how much relevant evidence each unit can gather before choosing a structure; a generic weekly conversion threshold is not a requirement to merge incompatible campaigns.

The collection disagrees about how much structure affects learning. It does not prove that campaigns share no learning, that structure is purely organizational, or that duplication is universally obsolete. Inspect current behavior and define why a proposed split or merge is needed.

## Verify audience boundaries

Heath, Piliero and Shiver discuss controls versus suggestions. Inspect the actual campaign workflow and saved audience configuration to determine what can expand and what is constrained. A historic toggle name cannot establish enforcement. Value rules express bid preferences where supported; they are not a substitute for a mandatory eligibility boundary.

Define acquisition, retention and reporting segments explicitly. A returning customer appearing in a prospecting report can reflect matching limits, definitions, timing or configuration, among other causes. Reconcile the evidence before asserting that an exclusion was ignored. Never change customer policy from another advertiser's anecdote.

## Build a meaningful creative portfolio

Tazkeer and Stenton organize concepts by persona, problem or awareness stage. Taylor tracks angles and mines actual customer language. Use the audience evidence available; do not manufacture personas or proof to fill a matrix. Distinguish a new idea from a variation in delivery or packaging so the team knows what it is learning.

Retain these production tools where they fit:

- **Pipeline A/B, Robinson:** reserve capacity for new concepts and for iterations of existing work. Choose the mix from the current bottleneck rather than copying a fixed ratio.
- **Angle × format batches:** combine a defined message with useful formats, then prioritize combinations the team can produce and evaluate. A complete Cartesian grid is not required.
- **4F, BitBranding:** fit, fabric, function and fashion prompt distinct apparel benefits. Adapt only where the product supports the claim.
- **Imitate, iterate, innovate:** learn a communication pattern, vary a supported concept, or test a genuinely new approach. Do not copy another brand's identity or evidence; an iteration need not be a measurable percentage different.
- **Organic trial, Diaz:** organic response can suggest candidates for paid testing. Organic winners are not paid-performance proof.

Balance creative capacity with the budget and observation window. The inherited ranges from a handful of concepts to large asset libraries describe different operations. No universal 20-ad minimum follows from the retained evidence. Do not pad the account with cosmetic variants just to meet a count.

Concrete historical configurations are retained in [method examples](method-examples.md) for research; use them as alternatives, not combined defaults.

## Choose a test design deliberately

The original catalogue offers several alternatives:

| Method | Useful purpose | What to decide before use |
|---|---|---|
| Control and weakest-link, Charley T | Compare a candidate against an established part of the portfolio | Fair comparison, relevant outcome and whether changing the control is warranted |
| Creative packs, Piliero | Track a batch by audience idea and concept | Budget allocation, delivery opportunity, observation window and interpretation |
| Batch production, Robinson | Coordinate a coherent set of angles and formats | Which variable each comparison can actually resolve |
| Test then scale, Shiver | Separate exploration from broader deployment | Added fragmentation, transfer conditions and whether results reproduce |
| Built-in testing workflow, Heath | Compare variants with supported experiment controls | Actual allocation, metric, duration and winner behavior in the current tool |
| Cadenced offer tests, Marin | Coordinate launches, analysis and follow-up | Appropriate lag, sample size, authorized spend and an honest definition of a winner |

Do not assume an ordinary ad set distributes spend evenly or that low-spend ads were disproven. A spending minimum may buy observations, but it also creates spend obligations and does not certify scalability. New test units can protect a control while increasing fragmentation. Adding to an existing unit can be appropriate; inspect the trade-off rather than imposing a never-add rule.

Record the question, metric, comparison, budget authority, decision window and stopping conditions. Hold unrelated creative transformations stable for an interpretable test. Inspect actual default metrics and enabled features instead of relying on old UI descriptions.

## Diagnose fatigue and pruning

Diaz and Taylor emphasize refresh pressure; Charley T emphasizes campaign management. Theriot and Davis warn about cutting apparent assist ads; Stenton argues for manual pruning. None of these accounts proves the cause of a new performance decline.

Examine frequency, audience reach, delivery changes, conversion delay, asset-level response and the downstream funnel. A declining metric can reflect creative wear, auction conditions, tracking faults, offer changes or ordinary variation. Judge individual assets and the aggregate campaign together. Low attributed conversions do not prove an assist role, and continued delivery does not prove the ad is valuable.

Use pre-agreed decision criteria and revisit the result after a justified change. Do not keep harmful spend running to obey a no-touch window, and do not repeatedly reverse changes on daily noise.

## Connect signal to business outcomes

Piliero, Asim, Stenton and Davis emphasize clean conversion data. Verify the event represents the intended action, with correct identity matching and deduplication where applicable. A visible CAPI label is configuration evidence, not an end-to-end result. Use the owning tracking skill for implementation.

Choose an optimization event from business relevance, data quality and conversion delay. Tier 11's early-event example illustrates the trade-off: an earlier signal can arrive sooner but needs evidence that it predicts the outcome sought. Do not universally ban proxies or assume they are equivalent to revenue.

Compare platform reporting with CRM, qualification and actual sales using aligned definitions and windows. Review new versus returning customers and attribution sensitivity. Neither click-only nor view-inclusive attribution is automatically causal truth. Revenue minus ad spend excludes other costs; label it accordingly rather than calling it full profit.

## Scale within economics and authority

Theriot, BitBranding, Chappell and Piliero offer different increment, cadence and spend-tier examples. Use them as research context. Set the actual decision from margin, cash constraints, capacity, evidence quality, reporting lag and the user's authorized budget. Spend thresholds do not prove when a feature becomes useful.

Increasing, holding, reducing or pausing may each be justified. No never-descale rule or never-set-an-end-date rule overrides the brief. Retain explicit spend limits and monitoring appropriate to the campaign; this reference does not authorize a new budget or schedule.

## Interpret case studies honestly

Shiver's reported near-equal lead totals do not establish equivalence without design and uncertainty analysis. Another creator's higher ROAS does not establish a universal winner. Vendor uplift figures describe their stated population and comparison, not the user's expected improvement.

For a recommendation, state the proposed setup, creative plan, evidence to gather, material trade-offs and allowed next action. Distinguish a configured tool, an offline plan and observed live behavior. Exact quotation, claims about internal systems and current product controls retain their own verification requirements.
