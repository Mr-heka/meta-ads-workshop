## Apply the evidence at its actual strength

These perspectives disagree about pruning, exclusions, production volume and automation. Preserve the disagreement as alternative hypotheses for a particular account. A repeated view or another brain's approval is not independent verification. Counts of followers, managed spend, client accounts or video engagement do not establish which method works.

Reported tests and case studies need their actual design, population, dates, settings, outcome definitions and uncertainty before being used as comparative evidence. A small difference in qualified leads does not establish equivalence, just as two different ROAS values do not prove one setup generally wins. Revenue minus advertising spend is a limited contribution measure; it is not full profit unless all relevant costs are included.

Numbers attached to creative packs, scaling increments, spending tiers, learning volume or refresh intervals are practitioner examples. Keep them with their source context when research needs them. Set an operational rule from the user's constraints and evidence, not the speaker's schedule. An instruction to never reduce spend or never set an end date does not override the user's budget authority.

Most retained examples concern e-commerce. Applying a method to lead generation requires an explicit translation through qualification, conversion delay, sales capacity and downstream outcomes. Do not silently substitute CPL for ROAS and call the result source-proven.

Exact product controls, API enums and model relationships require primary documentation or current account evidence. Creator references to Andromeda, GEM or another internal system do not establish that they are interchangeable. Historical employment, business results and current platform claims remain unverified beyond their actual source evidence.
