# Historical method examples

These details preserve the concrete shape of the inherited practitioner systems. They are research examples from the [original synthesis](../history/original/references/synthesis.md.original), not current Meta requirements, recommended spend, measured optima or permission to act. Creator labels remain inherited; locate the caption through [quotation evidence](quote-evidence.json) before direct attribution. The [decision reference](synthesis.md) explains how to evaluate fit.

| Inherited method | Original example | Fit question and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Charley T, control/weakest-link | A control plus one or two test ad sets; a 3×2×2 flexible-ad pattern | Can the comparison isolate a useful improvement without starving other work? The combination count is optional. |
| Piliero, M3/M4 packs | Label a pack by avatar/concept; spending minimum about one target CPA for at most seven days | Is that exposure affordable and informative? Minimum spend is an obligation, not a validation of scalability. |
| Robinson, Pipeline A/B | About 70/30 exploration/iteration when stalled; about 20–30/70–80 after winners | Which production bottleneck actually exists? The ratios describe a suggested allocation. |
| Robinson, batch matrix | Three angles by five creative types | Prioritize meaningful combinations; no need to produce the entire grid. |
| BitBranding, 4F and iteration | Fit, fabric, function, fashion; about 20% change as an iteration prompt | Relevant to apparel, with no validated metric for percentage difference. |
| Shiver, test then scale | A separate test campaign with a $100/day example, then move winners across formats | Check available budget, repeatability and transfer effects; this is not a minimum viable budget. |
| Marin, weekly cadence | Wednesday launch, Friday read; a ladder from $100 to $1,000 | Conversion lag and sufficient observations must govern the actual review, not weekdays. |
| Heath, testing tool | Two to five variants in the narrated workflow | Verify current allocation, availability and chosen outcome; an old UI walkthrough is not a specification. |
| Volume disagreement | Heath describes 20+ assets; Charley T roughly four to six concepts; Diaz roughly three to ten ads | Concepts and assets are different units. None is a universal quota. |
| Theriot / BitBranding, scaling | Examples of 20% increments versus 5% increments with different observation windows | Compounding and downside exposure matter; authorized economics can justify reducing or pausing. |
| Chappell / Piliero, spend tiers | A $30K/month transition example; one new weekly ad per $10K of monthly spend at scale | Spend alone does not establish the useful structure or production rate. |
| Learning and observation | Roughly 50 weekly events, a 72-hour pause in edits, and five-to-seven-day reads appear in the original | Historical guidance to investigate, not simultaneous requirements or reasons to ignore loss limits. |
| Attribution sensitivity | A 25% view-conversion warning appears in Marin's account | Inspect definitions and lag; a percentage alone cannot determine causal attribution. |
| Value rules | Narrated +30% or −90% bid adjustments for age segments | These are source examples, not verified current ranges or hard eligibility controls. |

These alternatives should never be assembled into one compulsory recipe. Preserve a coherent test design, define its failure condition, and change only what the user's authority and the evidence support.
