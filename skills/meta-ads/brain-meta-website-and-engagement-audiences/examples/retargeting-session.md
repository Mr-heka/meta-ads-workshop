# Worked example: a retargeting build session

A short Q&A showing how this brain answers. Every quote below is copied verbatim from `references/quote-library.md` with its original attribution. Nothing else is sourced.

## Q1. We have a pixel and some video content but no custom audiences. What do we build first?

Start with the foundational set, website first.

> "The first audience you need to build is the website custom audience, and it's probably the most valuable in your entire account." - Christian Jamal, *The ULTIMATE Custom Audience Guide for Meta Ads in 2026*

> "Go into ads manager. Click on audiences and create your foundational custom audiences including website visitors, Instagram engagement, Facebook page engagement, and customer list uploads" - Jon Rognerud, *The Facebook Ads Audience Mistake Costing You Thousands*

Set the windows to each source's maximum so the pools fill from day one.

> "So, if it's someone who has engaged with a lead form, for example, you can only do that within the last 90 days. Um with Facebook page engages, it's going to be 365. With website visitors, it's going to be 180." - Ben Heath, *Facebook Ads Retargeting Has Completely Changed*

## Q2. What watch percentage should the video-view audience use?

For a smaller account posting regular video, 25% across recent videos, with a minimum video length so the threshold means something.

> "If you have less than 50,000 followers and you share video content, whether frequently or otherwise, then you should be doing anybody who has watched any of my most recent 50 videos at least 25% of the way through." - Neil 'Shoney' Mac, *Facebook Ads Retargeting Audience Setup 2026 (Custom, Video & Website Retargeting)*

> "Uh, if you want to be more pinpoint with it, make sure that you don't select any videos that are less than 15 seconds long. So, what is 25% of a 5-second video, right? It's like 1.25 um seconds." - Neil 'Shoney' Mac, *Facebook Ads Retargeting Audience Setup 2026 (Custom, Video & Website Retargeting)*

Never build on the 3-second option.

> "a 3-second view tells you absolutely nothing about that potential lead. It just means their thumb paused on the feed before they kept scrolling." - Christian Jamal, *The ULTIMATE Custom Audience Guide for Meta Ads in 2026*

## Q3. How do we make sure the retargeting ad set actually stays on the warm audience?

Untick the suggestion toggle, or Meta treats the custom audience as optional.

> "and then you also, very important, want to make sure that you don't have use as suggestion ticked. If you do, then it's just going to function as the previous setup, it's not going to work in terms of only targeting your warm audiences" - Ben Heath, *Facebook Ads Retargeting Has Completely Changed*

## Q4. How should recency tiers and exclusions fit together?

Lead with the hottest recency tier, then exclude it from the tier below.

> "Sevenday stack is my favorite adset of all time. Okay, for easiness of understanding it's people who have visited your Instagram profile in the last seven days, people who have visited your Facebook page in the last seven days and people who have visited your website or any funnel in the last seven days." - Neil 'Shoney' Mac, *Facebook Ads Retargeting Audience Setup 2026 (Custom, Video & Website Retargeting)*

> "So, when you create adset number two and you target these audiences, you hit the exclude button and you put everybody that was in the 7-day stack in there. It just means that there's no overlap and confusion." - Neil 'Shoney' Mac, *Facebook Ads Retargeting Audience Setup 2026 (Custom, Video & Website Retargeting)*

And keep cold campaigns clean of everyone already in the funnel.

> "every cold campaign you're running right now should automatically be excluding three specific audiences: your existing customers, your existing leads, and anyone sitting inside an active retargeting funnel." - Christian Jamal, *The ULTIMATE Custom Audience Guide for Meta Ads in 2026*

## Q5. How do we check whether retargeting is doing anything?

Read the segment breakdown, not the campaign total.

> "go into your campaign inside ads manager and click on the breakdown. Then choose audience segments." - Jon Rognerud, *The Facebook Ads Audience Mistake Costing You Thousands*

> "If you're not reviewing segment breakdowns, well, you're basically optimizing blind here." - Jon Rognerud, *The Facebook Ads Audience Mistake Costing You Thousands*
