# Experts Mined: Meta website and engagement audiences (pixel segments, video views, engagers, recency)

## Ben Heath (popular)
The highest-authority voice in the set (28k+ views on this video, long-running Meta ads educator). Angle: post-Andromeda strategic reframe backed by live account data. Uniquely adds: the $80k campaign breakdown proving Meta auto-retargets inside cold campaigns (40% of spend to warm segments with zero custom audiences), the exact 90/180/365 lookback limits per source, the Advertising Settings step to define Engaged Audience and Existing Customers, and the full locked-retargeting recipe (further limit reach > switch setup > untick use as suggestion). Also the Hyros under-reporting caveat.

## Nick Theriot (hidden gem)
Small channel, top-decile engagement. Performance media buyer running ~$3,600/day on the account shown. Angle: retargeting lives inside one CBO with no custom audiences; the creative carries the awareness-stage signal. Uniquely adds: the 5-stages-of-awareness mapping onto retargeting creative, the five named bottom-funnel angles (sale, urgency, going-away, quantity break, new drop), the frequency-based method for spotting retargeting ads after the fact, and repeated ethics warnings on fake scarcity and fake authority. Note: his no-custom-audiences stance conflicts with our verified manual-control base; we keep his creative doctrine, not his targeting setting.

## Source quality notes

- **Usable sources: 8 of 10.** Two excluded: Weskill (thin, generic cold-vs-warm explainer, no Meta mechanics, unsourced "10x" claim) and Easy Click Fix (centred on customer-list CSV uploads, which is the list-audiences sibling's scope; only generic UI navigation was on-topic).
- **Only 10 sources were discovered at all**, well under the usual 25-40 for a brain. Retargeting-specific content inside the 12-month window is genuinely thinner post-Andromeda: the big channels have mostly folded retargeting into "one campaign" content, so dedicated audience-mechanics videos are scarcer. This brain leans harder on its two strategy anchors (Ben Heath, Nick Theriot) than a fuller brain would.
- **Freshness: no exceptions.** All 10 sources pass the 12-month rule.
- **Thin areas of the locked scope**: page-depth and time-on-site pixel intent segments got no direct coverage in any source (the sources treat website audiences as all-visitors or converters only); Instagram-engager mechanics beyond Jamal's interaction list are light; retargeting audience SIZING has no hard numbers in the set beyond Jamal's list-upload minimum and lookalike-spend tiers (which belong to siblings). Flagging rather than padding.
- Four hidden gems in the mix (Theriot, V.S.L, Rognerud, plus the excluded Weskill), so three of the four gems were usable.
