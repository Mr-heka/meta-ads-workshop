# Platform evidence and verification boundaries

## Reels creative and placement selection

[Meta: Instagram and Facebook Reels ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads), retrieved during the September 2026 rebuild, describes automatic and manual placement choices, placement asset customization and A/B testing. It recommends 9:16 video, audio and safe-zone protection for key elements. It also permits image creative in Reels. Its published performance comparisons concern particular tests, not guaranteed outcomes for another account.

Use the [Meta Ads Guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide) and the selected format's Ads Manager preview for current eligibility, dimensions, duration and size limits. The guide URL is a verification entrypoint; its individual specifications were not independently retrieved in this rebuild. Check the relevant page before asserting a numerical limit.

## What still needs account evidence

Exact controls, labels, supported placements and customization differ across workflows. Inspect the current objective, format and account before prescribing a click path. Read saved settings back after an authorized change and inspect delivered placement breakdowns when evaluating enforcement.

The inherited claims of a 15- or 90-second universal Reels ceiling, a fixed spending percentage for each excluded placement, API positions guaranteeing exclusion, and controls already applied to all live ad sets were not established by the primary page above. Retained transcripts are historical practitioner evidence; they do not prove today's account state.
