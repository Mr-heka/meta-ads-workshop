# Experts Mined - Paid media planning and forecasting (the CFO layer)

11 usable sources. Six are hidden gems (small channel, top-decile engagement, flagged Y in the source index built at mining time); five are stronger-reach channels selected on engagement, not raw views. Ordered by relevance to the CFO layer.

## Hidden gems (surfaced on engagement, not views)

### D2C Decoded By Nikhil - *I Audited 1000+ Meta Ad Accounts & Found These 9 Biggest Mistakes*
- Meta ad account auditor, 30-50 audits/month, India D2C context (rupee numbers).
- Angle: tracking accuracy and funnel diagnosis are the CFO-adjacent prerequisites before any budget decision makes sense.
- Uniquely adds: the mechanism by which broken tracking inflates the learning budget (under-reporting turns a Rs 10,000 learn into Rs 15,000-20,000), the full pre-scale checklist (3+ winners, 5-7 consecutive good days, CAC ceiling, wait 2-4 bad days), and the diagnostic-metric map (CPM/CTR/CPC/CPL for the ad layer, LP-view/ATC/checkout/purchase ratios for the site layer).

### Brotherdale - *Why You're Scaling Your Ads Wrong: CAC vs LTV*
- Media buyer, e-commerce.
- Angle: CAC is a range with a deliberate sweet spot derived from a full money model, and raising AOV/LTV is the highest-leverage way to expand allowable spend.
- Uniquely adds: the only worked CAC-range example in the set ($28/$40/$52 to $42/$61/$79 off a 1.5x AOV move = ~33% more allowable spend), plus the "meat on the bone" framing that underspending below the sweet spot is itself a mistake.

### Ads Daddy - *I Spent Millions on Facebook Ads. Here Are the Top Questions I Get From Beginners.*
- Practitioner, direct-response.
- Angle: literal working-backward arithmetic, CPM to reach to 1%/1% to minimum test spend.
- Uniquely adds: the cleanest break-even CAC / break-even ROAS derivation ($50 price, $10 COGS, $40 CAC, 1.25 ROAS), the 1%/1% minimum-spend rule with CPM sensitivity, the 5x-price kill threshold, and the offer-beats-creative proof ($97 to $58 to $26 cost per Typeform by changing only the offer).

### Joly Tematio - *Your Facebook Ad Budget is Probably Wrong - Do This Instead*
- Consultant/agency owner.
- Angle: budgeting is a discipline problem (too-small tests, too-fast max spend, chasing broken creative, ignoring the back end), not a single formula.
- Uniquely adds: the 75/25 proven-vs-new split rule (flipping to ~80/20 favouring testing from cold), the back-end tracking checklist (opt-in, VSL watch, scheduling, show, email), and the concrete scale-gate contrast ($50-70 CPA on $200 AOV = reinvest; $500 CPA on $250 AOV = negative-ROI red flag).

### Michael Tracey - *How Much Should You Spend on Facebook Ads for Your Pressure Washing Business?*
- Local-service (exterior cleaning) ads specialist, UK (pound numbers).
- Angle: the clearest working-backward CAC/close-rate/job-value math in the set, for local lead gen.
- Uniquely adds: the jobs-to-leads-to-spend chain (10 jobs / 10% close = 100 leads x £10 = £1,000), the point that improving close rate can beat raising budget (20% close halves the spend), the £25/day floor, and the ROI reframe ("would you spend £1,000 to get £20,000 back").

### DoneMaker - *CAC, Show Rate, Close Rate: The Numbers That Make or Break Scale*
- Agency-growth-lead interview, general B2B lens.
- Angle: CAC/show-rate/close-rate math as the accountability layer for setting realistic revenue goals and diagnosing scale-readiness.
- Uniquely adds: the full at-bat funnel sequence (leads to warm to booked to show to quoted to closed), the 30% close-rate heuristic where higher signals underpricing, and the token-test red flag ($5K against a millions-person TAM).

## Stronger-reach channels (selected on engagement)

### Ben Heath - *How to CRUSH Facebook Ads with a Small Budget in 2026*
- Widely-followed Meta ads specialist (largest channel in the set).
- Angle: small/tiny-budget operating rules, adjustment cadence, one-offer consolidation, the ROAS-vs-scale tradeoff.
- Uniquely adds: explicit small (<$3,000/month) vs tiny ($600/month or less) thresholds, the 7-10 day adjustment cadence, the consolidation math (5 ad sets vs 1), the willingness-to-pay-from-LTV framing, and the Ads Library long-runner signal (6+ months = probably working). Overlaps heavily with our verified manual-control base and is consistent with it.

### Christian Jamal - *How Much Budget Do You REALLY Need for Meta Ads (I spent over $100M on ads)*
- Big-agency-scale operator.
- Angle: formulaic per-ad and per-ad-set budget sizing off sale price and a risk-tolerance multiple.
- Uniquely adds: the 2.5x-sale-price-per-ad medium-risk kill ceiling, the whole-account formula (ads x ad sets = spend / half the sale price), the total-vs-per-ad budget distinction, and a free Google Sheet calculator that solves for any one of {ads, ad sets, budget}.

### Rich From Anywhere - *How Much To Spend On Meta Ads In 2026*
- Small-business/beginner-focused Facebook ads coach.
- Angle: the PPS (profit per sale) CAC-ceiling formula and the "how much can I afford" reframe.
- Uniquely adds: PPS = net profit / order count as a direct CAC ceiling ($500K / 1,000 = $500), the $10-20/day beginner cap, the "Core Four" (offer, creative, destination, follow-up), and an in-house-vs-agency cost comparison.

### Nick Boddington - *How Much Should You Spend On Facebook Ads?*
- Ads-clinic operator.
- Angle: the "50 optimised actions per week" rule for backing spend out of expected cost per result and structure.
- Uniquely adds: the clearest pacing formula in the set (£50 CPR x 50/week = £2,500/week = ~£350/day per ad set), the per-ad-set division warning, and the sharpest one-liner on consolidation ("Increasing budget does not guarantee better results. But consolidating budget always improves performance").

### Jason Hunt - *How Little Can You Spend on Ads? $5/Day Strategy Explained!*
- Generalist small-business ads coach, evergreen bucket.
- Angle: the literal "$5/day" question, tiny budgets as data-buying not lead-gen.
- Uniquely adds: the "$5-10/day is buying data" frame, the "at least 100 actions" Facebook learning threshold, the $30/$50 capability ladder (audiences/creatives at $30, split tests/funnels/retargeting at $50), and the separate $1-2/day evergreen video-views audience-builder.

## Source quality notes

- **Usable sources: 11** (above 8, so no shortfall on count). All 11 pass the freshness rule; none era-flagged; none flagged thin in the input notes.
- **Excluded: 1.** ElenIQ, *Lead Gen Forecasting Demo / Build Smarter Paid Media*, had no transcript (score 4.17, zero engagement) and was not mined. It was the only source that would have directly addressed forecasting-tool mechanics.
- **Thin sub-area: forecasting from spend.** The set is strong on working-backward math, small-budget floors, kill/scale rules, and the CAC/close-rate chain. It is thinner on formal forecasting (projecting a leads/sales curve from a spend curve over a campaign window with confidence bands). Ads Daddy's 1%/1% rule and Nick Boddington's 50-actions/week rule are the closest to a forecasting model; treat forecasting output as a point estimate from those, not a validated projection, and lean on our own live CAC once measured.
- **Geography spread:** rupee (Nikhil), pound (Tracey, Boddington), dollar (the rest). Currency in the quotes is verbatim; convert to AUD when applying to our numbers.
- **Consistency with verified base:** every consolidation, kill-rule, and scale-cadence claim here is consistent with `brain-meta-ads-manual-control-no-advantage/references/synthesis.md`. No source in this set conflicts with the verified base; where they touch the same ground (Ben Heath consolidation, judge-on-profit-volume) they reinforce it.
