# Trigger phrases and aliases (full recall list)

Moved out of the SKILL.md frontmatter to keep the opening a 60-second read.
Any of these should route to `brain-meta-local-lead-gen`.

## Load this brain when

- Building or reviewing a city-locked campaign (workshop seat fill, local event, service-area lead gen)
- Choosing a radius size, or wondering why ads serve outside the drawn radius
- A small-city audience is fatiguing (frequency climbing, CPL rising)
- Deciding instant forms vs landing page for local
- Designing qualifying questions to filter junk leads
- Setting local starting budgets, kill rules, or scaling pace
- Translating roofing/gym/home-service playbooks to event fill

## Typical trigger phrasings

- "fill the [city] workshop"
- "radius targeting"
- "geo targeting"
- "local lead gen"
- "leads outside our area"
- "lead quality is rubbish"
- "how big should the radius be"
- "city campaign is fatiguing"
