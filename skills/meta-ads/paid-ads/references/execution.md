# Platform and execution handoffs

Use the current skill router and available tools to select the domain owner. Resolve its path and read its current instructions before acting; a skill name or configured connection does not establish access. Keep project authority and canonical facts above a stale recipe. If a named owner is unavailable, use current official platform guidance with the available authorized UI/API or supplied export, and report the missing capability.

Common local owners include `google-ads-connector`, `linkedin-ads-pro`, `meta-ads-performance-operator` for Meta analysis, `meta-api-ad-builder` for Meta campaign changes, and `meta-token-renewal` for its access recovery. `ad-creative` owns creative production; `copywriting` owns landing-page copy. Check the router for tracking, audience or platform-specific work rather than assuming every referenced helper is installed. X and TikTok require discovery of their actual available lane.

## Before and after a launch or change

- Confirm account identity, business objective, dates/timezone, budget type and ceiling, conversion action, attribution settings, targeting intent, destination and intended campaign status.
- Verify the relevant tracking path with evidence appropriate to the task. Configuration or a pixel event alone does not prove a qualified lead, CRM outcome or purchase. Use a real conversion canary only when needed and authorized; a documentation review or creative draft can proceed without one.
- Check the actual creative and mobile destination against current placement specifications, approved facts and the offer. Inspect usable page behavior and meaningful performance evidence rather than treating one universal load-time threshold as a complete experience test.
- Preserve already approved changes and launch authority. If launch is not authorized, keep a proposed campaign draft or paused and record the pending decision. Paused status is not a substitute for authority to create arbitrary account objects.
- Record resulting IDs and read back settings/status. Check the relevant downstream outcome and report missing proof. On timeout or ambiguous write result, inspect current state before another write.

Credentials remain in the environment's approved secret manager. Never put tokens in the report, repository or a new convenience credential file. Billing setup, identity verification, policy declarations and owner authentication can require the account owner's action; continue independent work while that particular step is pending.

## Refresh volatile facts at use

Campaign types, bidding options, copy limits, targeting/exclusion controls, audience eligibility, attribution settings and policy requirements change. Use the exact feature's current official documentation and inspect its availability in the selected account. Historical source notes do not establish current access.

Two examples of why the old checklist cannot be copied unchanged:

- Google retired its old Similar Audiences product. [Google's sunset notice](https://ads-developers.googleblog.com/2022/11/announcing-deprecation-and-sunset-of.html) does not mean every lookalike feature is gone: [Demand Gen lookalike segments](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13541369?hl=en) are a distinct surface.
- [LinkedIn's discontinued-lookalikes notice](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a423698) supersedes the old instruction to create those audiences.

These official references were retrieved on 6 September 2026. Recheck current replacement behavior rather than turning a dated migration note into a new universal rule.
