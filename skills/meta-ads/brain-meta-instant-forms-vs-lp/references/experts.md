# Experts Mined, Meta instant forms vs landing pages (lead forms done right)

15 creators, ranked in the source index built at mining time by an engagement-weighted score, not raw views. 11 flagged as hidden gems (small channel, top-decile engagement); 4 are larger channels with strong engagement for their size. None is a household-name guru, this topic surfaces working practitioners, which suits a mechanics-heavy brain.

## Hidden gems (small channel, top-decile engagement)

### Smart Marketing Zone | Digital Marketing
- **Angle**: Practical post-Andromeda click-through for a local doctor client, with WhatsApp as the conversion location.
- **Uniquely adds**: The WhatsApp-destination mechanic (leads land in a chat, not a CSV), a Chat Builder FAQ auto-response template, and a clean statement of the Audience Network kill for lead campaigns.

### Godbless Iboyi (Growth With Paid Ads)
- **Angle**: Direct build-along of the instant form itself, framing the More-Volume vs Higher-Intent choice explicitly.
- **Uniquely adds**: The clearest single walkthrough of form mechanics, the mandatory-phone fix (Meta defaults phone to optional), live qualifying-question design, and greeting-screen congruence.

### Cardinal Digital Marketing (Lisa Fischer & Carol Costa)
- **Angle**: Agency case-study webinar on fixing Meta lead quality for healthcare, with hard before/after numbers.
- **Uniquely adds**: The deepest quantified source in the set, spam 57% → 0% via conditional questions then SMS verification; a single insurance-qualifying question cutting cost-per-member 44%; remarketing signal lifting qualified share 17% → 24%; statics beating video at the conversion stage.

### Joshua cmb | Adding Ally
- **Angle**: Home-services media buyer's operational mistakes list.
- **Uniquely adds**: The friction-as-dial philosophy (via Hormozi's quantity-vs-quality rule), the "any CTA that doesn't create a contact first is a bad CTA" rule, and the low-average-job-value economics gate.

### Tareq Istiaq
- **Angle**: Fast full-flow build-along confirming the current Ads Manager UI path.
- **Uniquely adds**: The OTP-as-friction-tradeoff opt-out reasoning, the Flexible Form Delivery off rule, and the Restricted-vs-Open sharing distinction. (Era note: uses local-currency examples; ignore the amounts.)

### Gavin Wiener (Funnel and Automation Operator)
- **Angle**: The forms-vs-LP choice framed as simplification vs control.
- **Uniquely adds**: The "instant forms take a whole variable out of the equation" framing, the case that LPs add a second CRO troubleshooting layer, and the default-to-forms-for-fast-signal recommendation for new campaigns.

### Rafael Hernandez (Great Marketing AI)
- **Angle**: Law-firm-vertical funnel with legal-compliance framing.
- **Uniquely adds**: OTP as the lead-form quality fix, TrustedForm/ActiveProspect as consent-evidence tooling, the thank-you-page bridge video, the 3-second mobile-load rule, and "cost per case" as the true metric. (Vertical-specific; core mechanics transfer.)

### Jacob Zwolinski (Lon Agency)
- **Angle**: The most numbers-dense side-by-side of forms vs landing pages.
- **Uniquely adds**: Explicit benchmarks, CPL $8-25 vs $20-60, answer rate 40-60% vs 60-80%, a clean decision matrix keyed on follow-up speed and price point, and the "cost per closed deal, not cost per lead" close.

### LASSO FRAMEWORK (Blake Ruff and Sherman Merritt)
- **Angle**: Gym/boutique-fitness agency podcast walking the Ads Manager UI.
- **Uniquely adds**: The Website+Instant Forms combined conversion location (dual-delivery), Meta's "25% better quality" OTP claim, and the Meta Pixel Helper verification prerequisite. (Era note: dual-delivery presented as newly launched; verify current.)

## Source quality notes

- **Usable sources: 15 of 15.** Comfortably above the 8-source floor. No source was thin enough to exclude; every one contributed at least one unique, on-topic point.
- **Freshness**: all pass the 12-month window. Two carry era notes: Tareq Istiaq (local-currency examples, logic transfers, amounts don't) and LASSO Framework (Website+Instant Forms combined placement and the "25% better quality" OTP figure presented as newly launched, treat as recency-flagged and verify before prescribing).
- **Thin sub-areas of scope (stated honestly, not padded)**:
  - **GHL integration** rests mainly on two sources (Hasib Ashad for field-mapping depth, Joshua cmb for the trigger/nurture pattern), reinforced by Dr. Matt Shiver's qualified-event wiring. Solid but not broad.
  - **The ToS / setup gotcha** is anchored by one explicit source (Hasib Ashad). It is confirmed and directly relevant to small-account setups, but only one creator states it plainly.
  - **Hard forms-vs-LP cost benchmarks** come mostly from one source (Jacob Zwolinski); treat his specific dollar ranges as one operator's numbers, not an industry constant.
- **Vertical skew**: several sources are vertical-specific (Cardinal = healthcare, Rafael Hernandez = law firms, Joshua cmb / Blake Bauer = home services). The form mechanics generalise; the CPL figures and compliance tooling do not.
- **Note on view counts**: raw views are very low on several hidden gems (single digits to low hundreds). Selection was engagement-weighted per the brain's method, so low views are expected and not a quality signal against them.
