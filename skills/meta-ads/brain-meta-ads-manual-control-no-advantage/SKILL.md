## What the experts agree on

1. **Creative IS the targeting.** Interest stacks and lookalikes are dead as primary levers post-Andromeda; the ad defines who Meta finds. (Fedotoff, Hunyor, Chappell, Fogarty, Denney, Blue Sense — Blue Sense: ~30% of any interest group is mis-assigned.)
2. **Consolidate, don't fragment.** Conversion data silos per campaign/ad set. Meta's stated learning-phase exit is **~50 optimisation events per ad set per 7 days**: 50 conversions/week through one ad set exits learning; the same 50 split across five never does. On small budgets: one offer, one campaign, one ad set. (Heath, Charley T, Pawliw, Blue Sense, Theriot.)
3. **Never touch what's working.** Do something next to the winner, never to it. Duplicate via post ID — keeps social proof, no learning reset. (Blue Sense, Pawliw, Hampton, Hunyor, Scalability School, Denney.)
4. **Kill/scale decisions on CPA/ROAS only.** CTR, CPM, hook rate diagnose why — they never decide what dies. (Heath, Charley T, Blue Sense.)
5. **The learning phase is conversion volume, not time** — and every edit resets it. Fixed optimization schedule, touch nothing more than every 7-10 days on a small budget. (Heath; Denney's 3-day no-analysis rule agrees.)
6. **Meta's settings defaults need manual disarming**: Advantage+ creative enhancements off, flexible ads off for testing, Audience Network out (at account level, see pre-flight), "use as a suggestion" unticked on retargeting lists, never edit live ads, schedule launches for midnight. (Blue Sense, Hunyor, Pawliw, Piliero.)
7. **Where automation genuinely wins**: delivery/audience-finding on broad, permutation matching inside dynamic creative, and budget routing across proven winners (CBO). Manual effort belongs in structure, test design, exclusions, and analysis.

## Named frameworks & methods

- **3-2-2 Method** (Charley T): 3 creatives × 2 headlines × 2 texts = 12 permutations, always under 20, in a dynamic-creative ad set.
- **3222** (Spencer Pawliw): extends 3-2-2 with 2 landing pages — Meta crawls and ranks your LPs for free.
- **Andromeda 1 / One Campaign Method 2.0** (Charley T): control ad set + test ad sets in one campaign, judged on profit volume (revenue minus spend), never per-ad attribution.
- **Pack system** (Sam Piliero): each creative round = a new ad set in the prospecting CBO, ad-set minimum budget = 1x target CPA, removed after 7 days max.
- **Hit-rate rule** (Scalability School): ABO if your test hit rate is high, CBO if it's low or budget is small.
- **Gladiators arena** (Scalability School): one scaling ad set, all proven winners, 7-day click, cost cap — breathes with weekend demand.
- **Test capacity formula** (Pawliw): daily budget ÷ target CPA = simultaneous tests you can afford.
- **Kill rules**: 1x CPA zero-conversion (Hunyor) / 2x avg CPA (Denney) / 2x AOV (Fogarty) / 3-5x target CPA (Blue Sense).
- **Champion CBO** (Ac Hampton): all proven winners from every campaign combined into one aggressive-budget campaign; never pause the sources.
- **Spend-tier ladder** (Piliero/Chappell): under ~3k dollars/month = one CBO, broad + 2 niche interests; under 30k = manual interest + structured testing still works; caps/day-parting earn their place at 100k+.

## Contrarian / disputed takes

- **Cost caps**: Charley T says they harvest buyers who'd convert anyway (cap at 10-20% of budget max); Bottom Line builds their whole launch-everything system on them; Scalability School splits it — never for testing, great for scaling. Blue Sense/Piliero: not below 100-200k dollars/month.
- **ASC/Advantage+ Shopping**: Theriot's controlled test had manual prospecting at 2.91 ROAS vs identical-ads ASC at 2.14; Heath and Fogarty say it's all a simple account needs; Charley T calls it a quality equaliser that drags every account to a C+.
- **Interest targeting under 30k dollars/month**: Chappell says you still need it (you're training the model); Fogarty/Fedotoff say broad-only from day one; Piliero splits it — 2 deliberately niche interest ad sets to steer the pixel, then broad.
- **Flexible ads**: Pawliw calls them "a terrible idea" (no per-creative data); Chappell uses them, but only to rapid-test 10 variations of one already-validated format.
- **Learning-phase patience**: Fedotoff says "if ads are good they'll work from day one" and kills intraday; Heath and Denney say don't judge before 3 days / stat-sig. (Fedotoff operates at 5-20k dollars/DAY e-comm scale — weight accordingly.)
- **Dedicated testing campaigns**: Denney's whole methodology vs Theriot/Charley T's "test winners die outside the main campaign." Denney herself concedes the isolated architecture needs 20k+ dollars/month.

## Execution playbook

- IF the account can't reliably exit learning THEN collapse to one campaign, one ad set, everything in it (per Charley T; Ben Heath's consolidation math agrees)
- IF an ad is winning THEN never edit or pause it, duplicate via post ID next to it (per Blue Sense Digital, Máté Hunyor)
- IF deciding kill or scale THEN use CPA/ROAS only; CTR, CPM, hook rate diagnose why, never decide (per Ben Heath)
- IF test hit rate is high THEN ABO; IF low hit rate or small budget THEN CBO (per Scalability School)
- IF launching new ads THEN schedule for midnight, or Meta dumps the day's budget into remaining hours (per Spencer Pawliw)
- IF retargeting a customer list THEN untick "use as a suggestion" or Meta turns the list into a broad seed (per Sam Piliero)
- IF testing creative THEN 3-2-2 dynamic creative, permutations capped under 20 (per Charley T)
- IF scaling proven winners THEN one ad set, all winners, cost cap, 7-day click (per Scalability School)
- IF view-through exceeds ~25% of conversions THEN switch to 7-day-click attribution (per Scalability School; Pawliw saw 7-10x reported collapse to 2-3x real)

Defaults: ~50 optimisation events per ad set per 7 days to exit learning (Meta's published threshold; several practitioners in this corpus quote lower numbers and others argue the threshold is folklore at small spend, but 50 is the stated default). Touch cadence 7-10 days on small budgets (Heath); no judging before 3 days (Denney). Test capacity = daily budget ÷ target CPA (Pawliw). Kill ladder: 1x CPA zero-conversion (Hunyor), 2x avg CPA (Denney), 2x AOV (Fogarty), 3-5x target CPA (Blue Sense). Hook rate >10% good, <5% poor (Heath). Frequency: cold ~2.5 max, warm 6-8 (Heath). Cost caps earn a place at 100-200k dollars/month (Piliero, Blue Sense); if used anyway, 10-20% of budget max (Charley T). Pack ad-set minimum = 1x target CPA, removed after 7 days (Piliero).

Pre-flight:
1. Advantage+ creative enhancements OFF (Blue Sense, Hunyor)
2. Flexible ads OFF for testing (Pawliw, Blue Sense)
3. Audience Network OUT (Hunyor's 14%-CTR accidental-click trap). **You cannot do this and keep Advantage+ Placements on in the same ad set:** unticking any surface switches the ad set to manual placements. Either exclude Audience Network at **account level** (Business settings > Brand safety and suitability) and leave Advantage+ Placements on, or accept manual placements. There is no ad-set toggle that does both.
4. "Use as a suggestion" unticked on every retargeting list (Piliero)
5. Existing customers excluded from prospecting (Piliero)
6. Launch scheduled for midnight, kill rule written down before spend starts (Pawliw)

Failure modes: editing a live winner resets learning, duplicate via post ID instead (Hunyor, Blue Sense). Fragmenting budget across ad sets starves every learning unit, consolidate (Heath). Killing ads on CTR or hook rate, decide on CPA only (Heath). Helicopter parenting, every edit costs ~48h of learning, hold the 7-10 day schedule (Heath). Trusting in-platform ROAS with heavy view-through, strip it before believing the number (Pawliw, Scalability School).

## Applied to your business

Feeds: write your ladder down with real numbers before applying any of this. Meta ads are the paid acquisition engine for `<your flagship offer + price>`, with `<your secondary offer + price>` as the other paid entry. If every front-end sale feeds a recurring offer (`<your membership or retainer + price>`), then the real metric is cost per front-end buyer, not per-ad ROAS. Lead-gen translation per the synthesis: swap ROAS for cost-per-lead against `<your target CPL>`.

Moves:
- Consolidate lead gen to one campaign, one ad set, so all conversion volume trains one learning unit (Heath's consolidation math against the ~50-events-per-week threshold)
- Test creative via Charley T's 3-2-2: 3 creatives x 2 headlines x 2 texts in one dynamic-creative ad set, never more than 20 permutations
- Write the kill gate down before launch: no kill verdict below a minimum spend, 7 days, and 3 conversions (a workable small-budget gate is `<2-3x your target CPL>` spent). Benchmark against your own account's 4-week rolling averages, never published industry numbers.
- Run the full pre-flight settings list on the ad account before launch. The Piliero "use as a suggestion" untick matters for any CRM-list retargeting, and exclude existing customers from prospecting.
- Set a fixed audit cadence and stick to it. Reviewing daily and acting weekly beats reacting to whatever today's numbers look like.

Does not apply at small spend:
- Cost caps and bid caps: Piliero and Blue Sense gate them at 100-200k dollars/month, far above a 2-8k/month account
- Fedotoff's day-one intraday kills: he runs 5-20k dollars/DAY e-commerce; small lead volume needs the Heath/Denney 3-day patience
- Bottom Line's launch-everything cost-cap system (150+ assets): built for mass UGC e-commerce pipelines, not a small lead-gen account
