# Example session: one worked Q&A grounded in this brain

Demonstrates how an answer should look when this brain is loaded: grounded in the

## User question

> The Sydney workshop campaign is on day 18. CPL was $3.89 in week one, now it's
> $23.64. Frequency on the cold ad set is sitting at 3.4. Should I pause the
> fatigued ad and push the budget up so it can outrun the spike?

## Brain-grounded answer

**Neither. Keep the winner live, ship rotated creative beside it, and hold the
budget flat while you check the off-platform causes.**

1. **Never pause the ad that built the history.** Mukuba's rule is the cleanest in
   the set: "You don't stop the ad that is doing well. No, no, you don't do that. What you do is you bring in more creatives".
   Pausing throws away the auction history the winner has earned (failure mode 3
   in the playbook). Retire ads only on a sustained CPL breach, and this one has
   breached, so the move is addition, not subtraction.

2. **This is creative fatigue, not an auction problem you can outbid.** Frequency
   3.4 on cold is past every band in the source set (Digital Prostuti acts above
   2; the verified base allows to ~2.5). CPL is the decision metric and it has
   breached the kill rule, so act now. But bidding or spending harder is the wrong
   lever: "The better your ad, the more meta will favor you over higher bids. So, don't obsess over manipulating bids. Obsess over making ads people actually want to respond to." (Jon Loomer).

3. **Rotate the surfaces Meta reads as new, next to the winner.** Digital
   Prostuti's rotation model: "You change the first three seconds of your creative. Change the thumbnail."
   Keep the proven body and CTA, swap thumbnail, hook, first 3 seconds and offer
   angle (problem / result / urgency / before-after). Ship the weekly 3-2-2
   concepts beside the winner; duplicate via post ID, never edit the live ad.

4. **Do not raise budget into a fatigued geo audience.** A city radius is a small
   audience by design: "If you take a small audience and give it a very large budget, the same people will keep seeing your ads over and over again." (Digital Prostuti).
   More spend here just re-serves the same people faster. Run Cavallaro's
   impression check (budget / CPM x 1,000) before any budget move; if the campaign
   is under ~1,500 impressions a day, consolidate ad sets instead.

5. **Run the off-platform checks before blaming delivery.** Playbook pre-flight
   step 7: confirm the spike is not seasonality, stock, landing page speed or
   tracking. A day-18 campaign is past the settle window, so edits are allowed,
   but only the additive ones above.
