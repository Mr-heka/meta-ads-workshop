# Experts Mined: Meta ads budgets, bidding and learning phase

17 creators across 22 videos (20 usable). Hidden-gem flags from the source index built at mining time (small channel, top-decile engagement).

## Konstantinos Doulgeridis: HIDDEN GEM
Heavy spender (5-10M/month across ~30 accounts). Angle: cost caps as an evergreen supplementary scaling tool held all year for demand spikes. Unique add: keep proven cost-cap ad sets alive in a healthy state between promos; BFCM case where cost caps lifted a $300K week to $388K.

## Manny Barbas: HIDDEN GEM (top video), 3 videos, 2 usable
Agency operator and brand founder. Angle: cost caps as a cash-flow and profitability tool with inflated budgets, patience over the 7-day window, campaign-level reading. Unique add: funnel-level CPA blending (ads at $44/$58/$38 averaging $33) and the frequency tell (1.7 vs 3.15) for funnel stage. His bid-caps video was era-flagged and excluded; note his usable case studies run inside ASC containers, which our doctrine bans.

## Cody Wittick: HIDDEN GEM
100% manual bidding agency at scale. Angle: five-mistake framework with exact formulas. Unique adds: the cost-cap ratio adjustment formula (miss by 1.5x, set the cap at 2/3 of target), the structural feasibility formula (cost cap x 7 x ad sets = minimum daily budget), the 15% change-reset threshold, and promo-period bid behaviour (underspend at sale start, overspend at sale end).

## Marin Istvanic: HIDDEN GEM
Media buyer with the cleanest testing/scaling split in the set. Angle: cost caps only for scaling winners imported by Post ID, never for testing. Unique adds: weekend scaling (inflated budget plus cap as permission to spend), the four-scenario cost-cap management matrix, and five argued reasons caps break creative testing.

## Andrei Lunev: HIDDEN GEM
Mechanics-first explainer. Angle: the exact auction behaviour of highest volume vs bid cap vs cost cap. Unique add: the cost-cap day-1/day-2 overspend failure mode with inflated budgets, and why he prefers bid caps because of it.

## Ben Heath: popular
The small-budget doctrine owner. Angle: defines small (<$3K/month) and tiny (<$600/month) tiers, slow the cadence, consolidate, niche hard. Unique adds: the 7-10 day optimisation schedule, Ads Library competitor modelling instead of paid testing, and the CPA-versus-LTV reframe.

## Chase Chappell: popular
Agency scaling mechanic. Angle: exact vertical scaling cadences. Unique adds: +20% every 3 days at ad-set level, dollar-step scaling past $500/day, and microbudget shifting between ad sets at flat total spend.

## Sam Piliero: popular
E-commerce funnel operator. Angle: CBO-always swim-lane structure (prospecting/retargeting/retention, ~86% to prospecting). Unique add: ad-set minimum spend = 1x average CPA for exactly 7 days to force new creative to get a fair read.

## Nick Boddington: popular
Post-Andromeda low-budget specialist. Angle: $20/day floors, 4+ genuinely different creatives, one broad ad set. Unique adds: Meta's creative similarity metric (same video, different overlay = same ad) and the mentality shift away from switching off every ad but the winner.

## Nick Theriot: popular
Single-CBO operator. Angle: one CBO per business goal, scaling purely by daily budget edits. Unique adds: the daily KPI check SOP (+20% on hit, 4-day patience on miss, -20% steps) and the "hard deck" spend floor that never gets breached.

## William Kast: popular, 3 videos
The most-used voice in this brain. Angles: auto-bid vs bid cap in live accounts, learning-phase myth-busting, and CBO top-spender defence. Unique adds: bid caps drain the warm pool (frequency spikes then spend stalls), the 2-3x target-cost judgement rule instead of waiting on learning status, the 3-day scale/7-day kill lookback split, and Meta's per-ad pre-evaluation against its ad database.

## Alex Djordjevic: popular
Hands-on bid tuner. Angle: iterative bid-cap tuning on a live $25K campaign. Unique add: the up-then-down loop (raise in sub-dollar steps until spend flows, then walk back down watching for "restraint" in the next 24 hours).

## AdAmigo AI: popular
Troubleshooting specialist. Angle: the underdelivery diagnostic ladder. Unique adds: the 200,000 audience floor, the sub-1,000-visitor pixel check, the forgotten-manual-bid trap, duplicate-to-restart, the temporary 2-3x AOV force-bid, and accelerated delivery as last resort.

## David Parrottino: popular
Auditor-style strategist. Angle: the full bid-strategy ladder with a staged rollout rule (highest volume until it breaks, then cost cap, then bid cap). Unique adds: target ROAS mechanics (7-day click only) and the platform-average-ROAS-is-2x warning against greedy targets.

## Build Your Ecomm: popular
8-year e-commerce buyer. Angle: mistakes-first cost-cap checklist. Unique adds: the $30K/month or 90-days-of-stable-data readiness gate and the cap formula (AOV x target margin % minus break-even cost per purchase).

## Derek Videll: popular
The calming contrarian. Angle: learning phase status is noise; budget increases actively hurt. Unique add: lowering budget is often the fastest way to raise ROAS (paraphrase; the transcript's exact garbled line is "lowering your budget is actually uh going to be the shest way to increase your rorowaz"), and treating learning limited as identical to active.

## Rafael Hernandez: popular
Checklist mechanic. Angle: learning phase as a system with thresholds. Unique adds: the up-funnel optimisation workaround to reach 50 events, batch edits at off-peak hours, and the CBO whole-campaign reset warning when adding ad sets.

## Christian Jamal: popular, EXCLUDED
ABO-to-CBO staged progression with one ad per ad set. Era-flagged: reads as pre-Andromeda doctrine presented without caveats and contradicts the consolidation consensus. Not used in the synthesis or quote library.

## Source quality notes

- **Usable sources: 20 of 22 videos** (17 creators). Two era-flagged videos excluded: Manny Barbas' bid-caps video and Christian Jamal's ABO vs CBO video. No sources flagged thin.
- **Freshness**: all 22 pass the 12-month rule. Nick Boddington carries an era note but is included because his video corrects pre-Andromeda habits rather than teaching them.
- **Thin areas, honestly**: target ROAS bidding rests on a single source (Parrottino). Accelerated delivery rests on a single source (AdAmigo AI), who notes the option was missing from the account he demoed. Cost-cap doctrine is rich (9+ sources); ROAS-goal doctrine is not.
- **Framing skew**: nearly every source is e-commerce/ROAS-framed. Only Ben Heath and Rafael Hernandez speak to lead gen directly. Translate ROAS talk to cost per lead against target CPA before applying to lead-gen accounts.
- **Known bias**: several cap advocates (Doulgeridis, Wittick, Istvanic, Barbas) operate at $30K-$10M/month. Their cap tactics assume conversion volume a small account does not have; the verified base (brain-meta-ads-manual-control-no-advantage) overrules them below ~$100K/month.
