# Sources and verification limits

Checked 5 September 2026. Meta's English pages were inaccessible to the research fetcher; official localized pages supplied dated evidence. No account, token, Ad Library query, browser session or creative was retrieved for this rebuild.

- [Meta Ad Library API](https://pt-br.facebook.com/ads/library/api): dated acquisition evidence and unresolved coverage conflict, detailed in the acquisition reference.
- [Meta release notes](https://pt-br.facebook.com/ads/library/api/releasenotes): historical field changes. Singular creative fields were retired in favor of lists; release history does not establish today's callable adapter or API version.
- [Meta public Ad Library](https://de-de.facebook.com/ads/library): a separate public search surface. Indexed/localized evidence is not proof of a current signed-in UI or successful query.

The installed tool metadata inspected during this rebuild exposed none of the old guide's ads_library_search, ads_insights_* or ads_get_creatives names. Discover capabilities at use. Public evidence does not measure conversions, asset rights or creative effectiveness. Local collection defaults and concept groupings are editorial choices, not Meta limits or measured benchmarks.
