# Research ads into useful concepts

Produce a sourced answer to the user's creative question. Separate what an ad shows, the pattern you infer and the hypothesis worth testing. Repetition is evidence of recurrence, not proof of spend, results or a winning concept.

The workflow is acquisition, teardown, clustering and, when requested, a creative brief or production. An analysis request ends with analysis. A request to build variations already authorizes that creative phase within its stated scope; do not insert another blanket approval step. Campaign changes, publication and spend need their own authorization.

## Establish the research scope

Use the brief and current context to identify the niche, target market and language, research question, relevant dates, supplied sources and requested deliverable. State reasonable assumptions and continue; ask only for a missing decision that materially changes the work. Do not default every market to the US.

Set a finite collection budget before acquisition. For an otherwise unspecified small exploration, a reasonable working bound is 30 unique ads, three query refinements and 20 minutes of collection, stopping at the first bound. These are adjustable local workload defaults, not Meta limits or a claim of representative coverage. Record the actual sample and stop reason. Honor a broader user-requested scope by planning it explicitly.

Create a new per-run folder inside the task's workspace. Keep supplied originals intact; do not overwrite an earlier run. Record source mode, query/advertiser set, market, date filter, collection time and sampling limits in a short run note. Read [acquisition and evidence](references/acquisition-and-evidence.md) before collecting data. Its dated source notes distinguish public UI, API and private metrics.

## Collect and inspect

Prefer the supplied material when it can answer the question. Otherwise choose a currently available source with the required market/category coverage and authorization. Discover actual tool names and schemas at use. The old `ads_library_search` name is historical, not an assumed callable tool or a reason to set up an account.

Use the relevant browser skill and its current interface when browsing is needed. Work serially by default. Delegate bounded read-only groups only when the host allows it and tab/session ownership is clear; a session label alone does not prove cookie or state isolation. Recheck the page after navigation or a modal changes it. Close only the task's own resources.

Inspect the creative itself where accessible. Record format, hook, body/offer structure, CTA, visual mechanism, stated proof and the audience tension it addresses. Paraphrase the mechanism and retain a safe source reference. A headline or page metadata is text evidence, not a visual teardown. Mark inaccessible, removed, obscured and partial records honestly; do not invent missing scenes, wording or results.

## Group and interpret

Deduplicate exact ad IDs within the source. Preserve distinct variants while grouping likely creative families, with a brief reason for each grouping. Distinguish unique advertisers from an advertiser's repeated variants. Similar templates across advertisers may come from an agency or supplier, so do not call them independent market validation.

Build a compact concept board. For each concept, show representative source IDs, observed mechanic, advertiser/variant counts within this sample, uncertainty, an alternative explanation and a differentiated idea to explore. Keep any ranking tied to the user's question, such as relevance, clarity or suitability for the intended audience. Explain that judgment instead of inventing a numerical performance score.

Ad Library observations cannot establish conversions or profitability. Where type/region-specific spend or reach fields exist, preserve their meaning, range, period and limitations. If the user requests actual account performance, route to [meta-ads-performance-operator](../meta-ads-performance-operator/SKILL.md) with the authorized account and metrics scope. Keep that private analysis separate from public research output.

## Produce only the requested next step

For a requested brief, turn the supported mechanism into original messaging and a distinct execution. Public visibility does not grant rights to reuse another advertiser's images, logos, people, testimonials or distinctive copy. Keep research citations while using owned or appropriately licensed production material.

Inspect every finished render at its intended size. Distinguish researched concepts, drafted variations, inspected assets and published ads. A good-looking render is not an effectiveness result or publication permission.

## Deliver a useful result

Return the concept board with attributable examples, sample limits, unresolved fields and the requested next action. Include a brief or assets only when requested. Keep private account exports and token-bearing references out of shareable deliverables. A zero-result or incomplete sample is a valid finding when its limits are clear.

Read [fictional decision cases](references/cases.md) for scope and evidence boundaries. [Source notes](SOURCES.These documents were reviewed locally; they do not prove live account access, browser behavior or creative performance.
