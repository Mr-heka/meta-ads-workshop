# Experts Mined: Meta message ads (Messenger, WhatsApp, IG-DM click-to-message ads)

14 videos mined 2026-07-06, all under 12 months old (freshness rule passed). Below, each creator with their bucket (hidden-gem vs strong-for-size), angle, and unique contribution.

## Hidden gems (small channel, top-decile engagement)

**Innocent Popka**, *How to Run Facebook Ads to WhatsApp Groups in 2025*
Beginner-focused. Unique contribution: the Traffic-objective-to-WhatsApp-group redirect trick, using the message template's "Visit website" CTA to push people into a WhatsApp group link, no autoresponder, avoiding WhatsApp bans. The clearest "cheap, no-tooling" path in the set.

**V.S.L Digital Marketing Hub**, *Facebook Engagement Ads for WhatsApp, Messenger & Instagram Messages*
Frames the category's core mistake (Traffic optimises clicks, not conversations) with a hard case study (50,000 Naira, 1,000+ clicks, <100 messages). Unique contribution: the only source that stacks and narrows interests deliberately (car-dealer, Engaged Shoppers → 875K) AND runs it as an Advantage+-vs-manual split test. Also the sober "messaging ads are not shortcuts" counterweight.

**Godbless Iboyi • Growth With Paid Ads**, two videos: *WhatsApp Group Beginner Guide 2026* and *WhatsApp Business Ads 2026*
High-spend media buyer, strong pro-broad ("zero targeting") stance. Unique contribution: names the three routes into a WhatsApp group (instant form, LP + pixel Lead event, click-to-chat); the create.wa.link pre-filled deep-link pre-framing tactic; the two-message-layer distinction (native CTA pre-message vs greeting message); and the midnight-start budget-pacing rule.

**Coach Isaiah**, *Facebook Ads + WhatsApp: Turn Clicks into Chats & Sales*, EXCLUDED (thin)
Despite the title, this is generic Facebook Page profile setup (contact info, hours, pricing tier), not a message-ad walkthrough. No objective, targeting, budget, or template content. Excluded from synthesis and quotes.

## Strong engagement for their size (well-known or mid-tier)

**HubSpot Marketing**, *How to Run WhatsApp Ads for Your Business (Full 7-Step System)*
The most systemised source. Unique contribution: the full 7-step lifecycle, concrete budget bands ($10-20 test, $30-50 fast learn), the three-tier chatbot stack (ManyChat / Chatbase / GPT Trainer), WhatsApp Status ads, and the only real compliance section (US template pause April 1 2025, block rate <5%, report rate <1%, quality rating targets).

**Jeremy Haynes**, *The Best Way To Run Instagram DM Ads In 2026*
High-ticket media buyer. Unique contribution: names "pixel conditioning" as the reason raw message optimisation decays, the true-cost-per-qualified-DM maths ($50 not $10), and two fixes (quiz-funnel-to-DM firing a web event; lead-objective campaign optimised on the Schedule event with the ad copy telling people to DM a trigger word).

**Justin Lalonde**, *How To Generate More WhatsApp Messages with Facebook Ads In 2026*
Agency owner, $20M/yr managed spend. Unique contribution: the budget formula (target CPA × number of creatives), the Wati/CAPI sync fix so Ads Manager shows leads/appointments/purchases instead of raw messages, and the warning that website-pixel matching to a WhatsApp campaign is "not one-to-one".

**Dr. Matt Shiver**, *How to Run Instagram DM Ads in 2026 (Post Andromeda)*
Practising media buyer, real client accounts. Unique contribution: the unique-keyword-per-ad method for tracing which ad produced qualified replies, the "messaging conversations started" inflation explanation (reported 21 vs 10 real), value-rule age down-weighting, the 2x-cost-per-call kill rule, and the Andromeda instant-form-to-DM chat sequence (completion vs disqualification messages).

**Joren Wouters**, *How To Connect a Facebook Ad to WhatsApp Automation (EASY)*
ManyChat-affiliated. Unique contribution: the full ManyChat build (keyword vs dedicated ad trigger, conversion-event logging per funnel step, Calendly URL-parameter pre-fill), plus the pro-message case (50%+ conversion, 90% open rate, automatic phone capture) and in-chat branching qualification with a low-ticket route for "no".

**My Online Master**, two videos: *Click-to-WhatsApp Ads Tutorial* and *How to Run Instagram DM Ads Using Meta Ads*
Agency-facing, India-market account. Unique contribution: the hardest line on WhatsApp Business vs personal (reports daily ban comments), native Chat Builder FAQ setup, and the IG-DM-as-digital-store framing for businesses with no website (privacy-policy-as-page-URL workaround, format-validated email/phone questions, disqualification paths).

**Amit Ghodke Videos**, *How to Run WhatsApp Lead Ads*
Agency operator. Unique contribution: a live client results dashboard (27, 78, 6, 51, 18 conversations started per creative for a wedding photographer), manual placement pruning (strip Audience Network + Messenger, keep FB + IG), and the vertical-aspect-ratio creative fix.

**Digital Growth Tutor**, *How to Create a WhatsApp Message Campaign in Meta Ads*
Fast condensed walkthrough. Unique contribution: the Engagement > "manual engagement campaign" path, and the "uncheck reach more people likely to send you a message" tip to keep delivery inside a strict geo.

## Source quality notes

- **Usable sources: 13 of 14.** One excluded as thin (Coach Isaiah, a Page-setup tutorial with no ad mechanics). Usable count is comfortably above the 8-source floor.
- **Freshness:** all 14 videos ≤12 months old; no evergreen exceptions were needed. No source pre-dates the 2025 Meta UI, so campaign paths (Engagement / Leads / Sales objectives, message destinations) reflect current Ads Manager.
- **One era caveat, retained:** My Online Master's WhatsApp video runs on an India-market ads account, so a few UI labels (Special Ad Category framing, budget-scheduling percentages) are region-flavoured. The underlying WhatsApp-destination mechanics are current, so the video is kept with that note rather than excluded.
- **Thin sub-areas of the scope (stated honestly, not padded):**
  - **Messenger-only ads** are under-covered. The set is heavily WhatsApp and IG-DM; Messenger appears mostly as a placement that creators *uncheck*, not as a primary destination. Treat Messenger-specific guidance as extrapolated from the WhatsApp/IG mechanics.
  - **CAPI-for-messaging plumbing** is covered at the principle level (Lalonde/Wati, Haynes/web-event) but no source walks the actual server-side event payload. For that depth, pair with `brain-meta-capi-server-side-deep`.
  - **Cost dynamics** (CPM/CPL benchmarks) are sparse and currency-mixed (Naira, INR, USD), usable for method and ratios, not for absolute AUD benchmarks.
- **Spend-claim caution:** Godbless Iboyi cites very large managed-spend figures; treat as unverified positioning, weight the method not the number.
