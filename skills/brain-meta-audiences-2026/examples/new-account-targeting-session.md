# Worked session: should I fill detailed targeting on this new account?

A short session showing this brain answering a live question. Every quote below
is copied verbatim from `references/quote-library.md`; nothing is paraphrased.

## The question

A fresh ad account, zero conversion history. Fill in detailed targeting or go
straight broad?

## The session

**Step 1: establish the baseline rule.** On mature accounts the consensus is
that the suggestion layer no longer steers delivery.

> "for most advertisers now, Meta is just better at targeting than we are advertisers" - Ben Heath, *Is Meta Ads Targeting Still Needed in 2026?*

> "we've had an ad set that's added in a bunch of stuff into that suggestion audience section versus one where we haven't added anything in... it's basically identical" - Ben Heath, *Is Meta Ads Targeting Still Needed in 2026?*

**Step 2: check whether the baseline applies here.** It does not bite yet.
This account has no conversion data, which is the one situation where the
suggestion layer still earns its keep. Heath frames the whole layer as
optional signal, not a fence:

> "everything that you add in suggest an audience. That's what I'm referring to here. Take it, leave it. Does it provide some benefit? Maybe not a lot." - Ben Heath, *Is Meta Ads Targeting Still Needed in 2026?*

And Chappell puts small, young accounts on the manual side of his ladder:

> "This is what we call the transition period. And if you're spending under 30k per month, you need targeting." - Chase Chappell, *How To ACTUALLY Target with Facebook Ads (Post-Andromeda Update)*

**Step 3: set the controls that actually bind, before touching suggestions.**

> "any of the targeting options that you select within the control section, those are hard constraints, hard boundaries" - Ben Heath, *Is Meta Ads Targeting Still Needed in 2026?*

> "There are really only a few things most businesses should use as hard controls." - LYFE Marketing, *The ONLY Meta Ads Targeting Tutorial You Need in 2026*

**Step 4: keep the interest layer thin so learning is not starved.**

> "When your budget gets split across too many audiences, meta gets less data in each pocket. That slows down meta's learning, and that slows optimization, making scaling harder." - LYFE Marketing, *The ONLY Meta Ads Targeting Tutorial You Need in 2026*

**Step 5: put the real targeting in the creative from day one.**

> "you pretty much get what you ask for when it comes to running meta ads, but you have to call out your ideal customer within the first 10 seconds of the ad." - Ash Davis, *How To Target Business Owners on Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)*

## The answer

Yes, fill it, temporarily. One or two interest signals close to the buyer as a
starting nudge, never a stack. Hard controls (location, age floor, gender,
exclusions) set properly first, because those are the only fields Meta treats
as boundaries. Creative carries the persona callout from the first ad. Once
conversions flow steadily, empty the suggestion layer and run broad, per the
playbook rule in `SKILL.md`: new account fills detailed targeting as a starting
signal and removes it once conversions flow (Heath).
