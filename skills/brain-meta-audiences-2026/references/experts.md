# Experts Mined: Meta ads audiences 2026 (the full audience system survey)

## Ben Heath: popular

- **Angle**: veteran agency operator, advertising on Meta since 2013, considered and evidence-led rather than hype-led.
- **Uniquely adds**: the Controls versus Suggest-an-Audience distinction (hard constraints versus ignorable signals), the 180-day website custom audience lookback cap that makes uploaded lists irreplaceable, the new-account versus mature-account split on whether suggestions help, Value Rules as the emerging optimisation lever, and the £96,000 versus £58,000 unreported-revenue attribution example.

## Sam Piliero: popular (two videos in this set)

- **Angle**: agency operator (The Moonlighters), structural doctrine with real spend numbers.
- **Uniquely adds**: the N3 system (broad-pack CBO plus single-interest ad sets one step removed) with the $46k to $282k spend scale case; the 180 million to 10-12 million audience-size trade-off; the 30/90/all-time exclusion window stack; and the Hormozi-era avatar doctrine (demographic in the ad steers delivery, per-angle ROAS floors, creative volume requirement).

## Chase Chappell: popular

- **Angle**: large-scale media buyer, spend-tiered playbooks.
- **Uniquely adds**: the $30k/month threshold doctrine (under = interest testing to seed the algorithm, over = CBO broad by product, $500k+ = full automation), plus creative angles mapped to buyer awareness stages, with account numbers at both tiers ($8.8k at 5x, $40k at 5.5x).

## Ash Davis: popular (smaller channel, strong engagement)

- **Angle**: B2B lead-gen agency owner whose own market is business owners.
- **Uniquely adds**: the only current-source coverage of targeting business owners: call out the exact customer in the first 3 to 10 seconds, behavioural language over identity labels, and pre-qualification forms that stop bad-fit leads training the algorithm. Also the Meta-plus-YouTube compounding claim.

## LYFE Marketing: popular (agency channel)

- **Angle**: agency 2026-update voice, codifies the simplified doctrine.
- **Uniquely adds**: the hard-controls-only rule (location, age, gender, nothing else), the three jobs of a custom audience (retarget, exclude, feed data), the four starter custom audiences, bigger-window-beats-tight-window retargeting, and the one-campaign two-ad-set account structure.

## Innocent Popka: hidden gem (flagged in sources index), ERA-FLAGGED

- **Angle**: Nigeria-focused practitioner walkthrough for reaching high-income buyers.
- **Adds (legacy only)**: a complete manual interest-stack recipe for premium segments (luxury goods, frequent international travellers, engaged shoppers, age 28 to 55, 20-city cap) and one durable creative rule: premium buyers only respond to premium creative. Excluded from doctrine because it ignores the Advantage+/Andromeda shift entirely.

## Fosters Marketing: hidden gem (flagged in sources index), THIN

- **Angle**: generic 10-minute framework listicle.
- **Adds**: nothing operational; buzzword-level mentions of Advantage+, custom audiences and lookalikes. Excluded from all synthesis themes.

## Param Digital Marketer: neither popular nor gem (lowest-scored source), ERA-FLAGGED

- **Angle**: fundamentals explainer of the classic saved/custom/lookalike funnel.
- **Adds (vocabulary only)**: the cleanest cold = saved, warm = custom, hot = converters mapping, plus install-the-pixel-before-spending logic. Treated as the textbook baseline the current sources react against, not as 2026 doctrine.

## Source quality notes

- **This is a survey brain by design: only 9 sources.** The deep-dive siblings (website/engagement audiences, list audiences and CRM sync, lookalikes and seeds, exclusion architecture, local lead gen) carry the depth on each sub-topic.
- **Usable sources for doctrine: 6 of 9** (Piliero x2, Heath, Chappell, Davis, LYFE Marketing). That is below the usual 8-source target, stated plainly. The 3 exclusions: Fosters Marketing (thin), Innocent Popka and Param Digital Marketer (era-flagged, pre-Andromeda methods presented as current).
- **Freshness**: all 9 sources pass the 12-month rule applied at discovery (build date 2026-07-05). No exceptions.
- **Hidden gems**: the sources index flags 2 gem sources (Popka, Fosters); the discovery metadata claimed 3, corrected to 2 in the SKILL header to match the scored table. Both gems ended up excluded from doctrine, so the usable set is all mainstream-practitioner voices.
- **Thin areas**: business-owner and premium-segment targeting rests on one usable source (Ash Davis); Popka covers it only as legacy. Audience size doctrine has no dedicated source, it is assembled from LYFE and Piliero side-points. Cold/warm/hot terminology relies on an era-flagged source for definitions.
- **Cross-check**: every doctrine theme was checked against the 24-source evidence in `brain-meta-ads-manual-control-no-advantage/references/synthesis.md`. Two conflicts found and flagged in synthesis.md (Chappell's Advantage+ container with flexible creatives; Piliero leaving Advantage+ on with all placements open). The stricter rule holds both.
