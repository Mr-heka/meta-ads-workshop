# Experts Mined — Higgsfield AI image prompting

26 videos, selected on engagement + substance (not raw views). 3 surfaced as hidden gems (small channel, top-decile engagement). Listed by what each uniquely adds.

## Hidden gems (small channel, high signal)

- **Artturi Jalli** — Exhaustive feature-by-feature tour with honest cost analysis. Best on Soul ID, Mood Board, Cinematic Cameras, Canvas, Relight/Upscale, reference-image headshots, and which built-in tools to skip (rates face-swap and inpaint poor).
- **metricsmule** — Nano Banana Pro 4K hands-on + realism meta-tokens. Celebrity/landmark/text accuracy tests, reverse-engineering a look from an output image, reference-image prompting patterns.
- **Dylan Davis** — The JSON prompt system. The 5-input creative-director framework, Google-blog-distilled system prompt, surgical in-context edits, and the "context beats jargon" claim for Nano Banana Pro.

## Popular experts (high reach + solid engagement)

- **AI Master** — The most systematic teacher. Two videos (Nano Banana + GPT Image 1.5) covering the six-component formula and subject-specific recipes: portrait, product, architecture, landscape, street, text-in-image, abstract, style transfer, background replacement.
- **Taylor Bay Studios** — The Nano Banana formula applied to editing: the five action words (Add/Change/Make/Remove/Replace) and clothing-swap-without-face-drift.
- **Higgsfield AI (official)** — First-party prompt-engineer knowledge: the 21:9 anamorphic tip, the Soul Cinema → Seedance pipeline, MCP/Claude content factory, "AI Videos Look Bad" diagnostics (start frame = 80%).
- **Rourke Heath** — Nano Banana Pro as the backbone of a Claude-CLI production pipeline: product sheets, expression sheets, style-reference character generation, UUID references. (Two videos.)
- **DGI Kaos** — Broadest Higgsfield image feature tour: Shots, Popcorn, Skin Enhancer, Soul ID photo protocol, model comparison, "no text on clothing" AI-tell suppression.
- **Jack Vs. AI** — VFX pro testing Higgsfield vs MidJourney: which style presets to use vs avoid, the Recreate extension, ChatGPT reverse-engineering, honest artifact reporting.
- **Joshua Mayo** — Clear walkthrough of presets, Cinema Studio vs presets, and Soul character creation for branded work.
- **AI Foundations** — The Claude × Higgsfield MCP/CLI setup: model auto-selection, photographic vocab auto-injection, thumbnail automation with a reference-image library.
- **Theoretically Media** — Filmmaker's eye: Shotdeck workflow, 21:9 framing, ~2,800-char prompt discipline, camera/lens keyword sourcing.
- **Sebastien Jefferies** — Nano Banana Pro "Angles 2.0" (12-angle generation) feeding Kling Elements.
- **David Manning** — Cinema Studio Cast feature, color grading controls, start/end frames.
- **Matt Loui** — Character expression sheets and reference-driven consistency for downstream video.
- **NetworkChuck** — General LLM prompting foundations (persona/context/few-shot/CoT) that govern prompt-template quality.
- **Joseph Martin** — Beginner overview; GPT Image vs Higgsfield Soul distinction, template-recreation workflow.
- **WealthWise** — JSON prompting for Nano Banana Pro and face consistency across shots.
- **AI Video School** — The "train a GPT on the model docs, stop hand-writing prompts" doctrine.
- **Tao Prompts** — Image-first workflow for downstream character consistency; negative/anchor prompting.

## Caveats
- **NetworkChuck, AI Video School, Tao Prompts** are general or video-leaning; included for the prompting principles that transfer to image work.
- A couple of sources (Yaroflasher-style reviews) are platform critique, used only for credit/plan and privacy risk context.
- **FLUX, Reve, Seedream, GPT Image 1.5 advanced prompting** are thin in this corpus; the formula and editing rules transfer, but model-specific deep dives would need separate sourcing.
